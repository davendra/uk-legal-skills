# Jurisdiction: England & Wales

All analysis produced by UK Legal Skills is scoped exclusively to the laws of **England and Wales**. This is not a UK-wide tool -- Scots law and Northern Ireland law are explicitly out of scope.

## Why England & Wales Only

England and Wales share a single legal system that is distinct from Scotland (which has its own court system, land law, and contract law principles) and Northern Ireland (which has separate legislation in many areas). Mixing jurisdictions would produce unreliable analysis.

::: warning
Never add references to Scottish statutes (e.g., Land Registration (Scotland) Act 2012) or Northern Irish equivalents. Every skill file pins England & Wales as the jurisdiction.
:::

## Key Legislation by Category

::: warning 2025 reforms commence in phases
The 2025 Acts in the tables below — the **Employment Rights Act 2025**, the **Renters' Rights Act 2025** (first major phase from 1 May 2026 per GOV.UK's roadmap), and the **Data (Use and Access) Act 2025** — are enacted but commence in phases, so not every provision binds today. The skills run live in-force checks via the `uk-legislation` MCP server (`check_in_force`, `check_amendments`) at analysis time and label each finding current, transitional, or prospective. See the [legislation reference](/reference/legislation) for the per-Act detail.
:::

## Common Law Principles

UK Legal Skills also references key common law principles where applicable:

- **Banks v Goodfellow (1870)** -- testamentary capacity test for wills
- **Restraint of trade doctrine** -- reasonableness of restrictive covenants
- **Unfair prejudice** (Companies Act 2006, s.994) -- minority shareholder protection
- **Hadley v Baxendale** -- remoteness of damage in contract
- **Caparo Industries v Dickman** -- duty of care test
