## 1. DEFINITIONS AND INTERPRETATION

### 1.1 Definitions

In this Lease, unless the context otherwise requires:

**"1954 Act"** means the Landlord and Tenant Act 1954;

**"Authorised Use"** means use as offices within Class E(g)(i) of the Town and Country Planning (Use Classes) Order 1987 (as amended);

**"Break Date"** means 14 March 2029;

**"Commencement Date"** means 15 March 2026;

**"Common Parts"** means the entrance hall, stairways, lifts, corridors, WC facilities, and other parts of the Building provided by the Landlord for common use;

**"Insured Risks"** means fire, storm, tempest, flood, lightning, explosion, earthquake, aircraft, riot, civil commotion, malicious damage, subsidence, ground heave, impact, burst pipes, and such other risks as the Landlord may from time to time insure against;

**"Interest Rate"** means 4% per annum above the base rate of Barclays Bank plc from time to time;

**"Landlord's Surveyor"** means a surveyor appointed by the Landlord, being a member of the Royal Institution of Chartered Surveyors;

**"Permitted Hours"** means 07:00 to 22:00 on any day;

**"Planning Acts"** means the Town and Country Planning Act 1990 and the Planning (Listed Buildings and Conservation Areas) Act 1990;

**"Property"** means Unit 4, Ground Floor, 120 Clerkenwell Road, London EC1R 5DP, as more particularly described in Schedule 1;

**"Rent Commencement Date"** means 15 March 2026;

**"Review Dates"** means the third anniversary of the Commencement Date;

**"Service Charge"** means the sums payable by the Tenant pursuant to Schedule 3;

**"Service Charge Cap"** means GBP 8,000 per annum (exclusive of VAT);

**"Term"** means the term of 5 years from and including the Commencement Date;

**"VAT"** means value added tax as provided for in the Value Added Tax Act 1994.

### 1.2 Interpretation

(a) References to statutes include any statutory modification, re-enactment, or regulations made under them.

(b) References to the Landlord and Tenant include their respective successors in title.

(c) Where the Tenant comprises two or more persons, obligations are joint and several.

(d) Headings are for convenience and do not affect interpretation.

## 2. DEMISE AND TERM

2.1 The Landlord demises the Property to the Tenant for the Term, together with the rights set out in Schedule 1, excepting and reserving to the Landlord the rights set out in Schedule 2, subject to and with the benefit of the matters referred to in the Prescribed Clauses.

2.2 This Lease is a lease to which the provisions of sections 24 to 28 of the 1954 Act apply. The Tenant's right to a new tenancy under the 1954 Act is **not** excluded by virtue of section 38A of that Act.

## 3. RENT

### 3.1 Annual Rent

The Tenant shall pay to the Landlord the annual rent of **GBP 45,000** (Forty-Five Thousand Pounds) exclusive of VAT, payable by equal quarterly instalments in advance on the usual quarter days (25 March, 24 June, 29 September, and 25 December), the first payment being a proportionate sum in respect of the period from and including the Rent Commencement Date to the day before the next quarter day.

### 3.2 Method of Payment

All rent shall be paid by banker's standing order or direct debit to such account as the Landlord may from time to time nominate in writing.

### 3.3 Rent Review

The rent shall be reviewed on the Review Date in accordance with Schedule 4 (Rent Review).

### 3.4 Interest on Late Payment

If any rent or other sum payable under this Lease remains unpaid for 14 days after the due date (whether formally demanded or not), the Tenant shall pay interest on the outstanding amount at the Interest Rate, calculated on a daily basis from the due date until payment.

## 4. TENANT'S COVENANTS

The Tenant covenants with the Landlord:

### 4.1 Rent

To pay the rent and all other sums due under this Lease at the times and in the manner specified without any deduction, counterclaim, or set-off.

### 4.2 Rates and Outgoings

To pay all present and future rates, taxes, duties, charges, assessments, impositions, and outgoings payable in respect of the Property (whether assessed on the owner or occupier), excluding any tax on the rental income of the Landlord.

### 4.3 Repair

To keep the Property (including the Landlord's fixtures and fittings, the interior surfaces of external walls, window glass, and all conduits serving the Property exclusively) in good and substantial repair and condition throughout the Term, damage by the Insured Risks excepted (unless the insurance is vitiated by the act or default of the Tenant).

### 4.4 Decoration

To decorate the interior of the Property in a good and workmanlike manner in the last year of the Term and at intervals of not less than three years during the Term, using materials of good quality.

### 4.5 Alterations

(a) Not to make any structural alterations or additions to the Property.

(b) Not to make any non-structural alterations or additions to the Property without the prior written consent of the Landlord (such consent not to be unreasonably withheld or delayed), provided that the Tenant submits plans and specifications and complies with all conditions reasonably imposed by the Landlord.

### 4.6 Use

(a) To use the Property only for the Authorised Use.

(b) Not to use the Property for any illegal or immoral purpose.

(c) Not to do anything on the Property that may be or become a nuisance, annoyance, or cause of damage to the Landlord or the occupiers of neighbouring property.

### 4.7 Insurance

(a) Not to do or omit anything that may render any insurance policy on the Property void or voidable or increase the premium payable.

(b) To comply with all requirements of the insurers.

### 4.8 Compliance with Laws

To comply with all statutes, statutory instruments, regulations, bye-laws, and codes of practice relating to the Property and its use, including (without limitation) the Regulatory Reform (Fire Safety) Order 2005, the Health and Safety at Work etc. Act 1974, and the Equality Act 2010.

### 4.9 Alienation

(a) Not to assign, underlet, charge, or part with or share possession or occupation of the whole or any part of the Property without the prior written consent of the Landlord (such consent not to be unreasonably withheld or delayed in accordance with section 19(1A) of the Landlord and Tenant Act 1927).

(b) In the case of any permitted assignment, the assignee shall enter into an authorised guarantee agreement in the form required by the Landlord.

### 4.10 Yielding Up

At the expiry or sooner determination of the Term, to yield up the Property in the state of repair and condition required by this Lease, having removed all tenant's fixtures and fittings and made good any damage caused by their removal.

---

## 5. LANDLORD'S COVENANTS

The Landlord covenants with the Tenant:

### 5.1 Quiet Enjoyment

That the Tenant, paying the rent and performing the covenants in this Lease, shall peaceably and quietly hold and enjoy the Property during the Term without any lawful interruption by the Landlord or any person claiming under or in trust for the Landlord.

### 5.2 Insurance

To insure the Building (including the Property) against the Insured Risks in the full reinstatement value with a reputable insurer and to use all reasonable endeavours to procure reinstatement in the event of damage or destruction by an Insured Risk.

### 5.3 Services

To provide the services set out in Schedule 3, subject to the Service Charge provisions.

### 5.4 Structure and Exterior

To keep the structure and exterior of the Building (including the roof, foundations, external walls, and Common Parts) in good and substantial repair.

---

## 6. SERVICE CHARGE

### 6.1

The Tenant shall pay the Service Charge as a contribution towards the costs and expenses incurred by the Landlord in providing the services and maintaining the Common Parts, as set out in Schedule 3.

### 6.2 Service Charge Cap

The Tenant's total liability for Service Charge in any accounting year shall not exceed the **Service Charge Cap of GBP 8,000** (exclusive of VAT). For the avoidance of doubt, any costs exceeding the Service Charge Cap shall be borne by the Landlord.

### 6.3 Reconciliation

The Landlord shall provide the Tenant with a certified summary of the service charge expenditure within 4 months of the end of each accounting year, together with a reconciliation statement. Any overpayment shall be credited against the next quarterly payment.

---

## 7. FORFEITURE

### 7.1

Without prejudice to any other right or remedy of the Landlord, if and whenever during the Term:

(a) the rent or any part thereof is unpaid for 21 days after becoming payable (whether formally demanded or not); or

(b) the Tenant is in breach of any covenant or obligation in this Lease; or

(c) the Tenant (being a company) enters into liquidation (whether voluntary or compulsory, but not a solvent voluntary winding up for the purposes of amalgamation or reconstruction), or has a receiver, administrator, or administrative receiver appointed, or enters into a company voluntary arrangement under Part I of the Insolvency Act 1986;

then, subject to the provisions of section 146 of the Law of Property Act 1925 (where applicable), the Landlord may re-enter the Property (or any part thereof in the name of the whole) and thereupon the Term shall absolutely determine, but without prejudice to the rights and remedies of the Landlord in respect of any antecedent breach.

---

## 8. BREAK CLAUSE

### 8.1 Mutual Break Right

Either party may determine this Lease on the **Break Date (14 March 2029)** by giving to the other party not less than **6 months' prior written notice** expiring on the Break Date.

### 8.2 Conditions Precedent (Tenant's Break)

The Tenant's right to break is conditional upon:

(a) vacant possession of the Property being given to the Landlord on the Break Date;

(b) the Tenant having paid all rent and other sums due under this Lease up to the Break Date; and

(c) there being no subsisting material breach of the Tenant's covenants in this Lease at the Break Date.

### 8.3 Conditions Precedent (Landlord's Break)

The Landlord's right to break is conditional upon the Landlord paying to the Tenant a sum equivalent to 3 months' rent as compensation for early termination.

## SCHEDULE 1 -- Description of the Property and Rights Granted

### Part 1 -- The Property

Unit 4, Ground Floor, 120 Clerkenwell Road, London EC1R 5DP, comprising approximately 1,800 square feet (167 square metres) of office space as shown edged red on the attached plan.

### Part 2 -- Rights Granted

1. The right to use the Common Parts for access to and egress from the Property.
2. The right to use the WC and kitchen facilities on the ground floor.
3. The right to use 4 car parking spaces in the rear car park as designated by the Landlord.
4. The right of support and shelter from the remainder of the Building.
5. The right to the passage of utilities through conduits serving the Property.

---

## SCHEDULE 2 -- Rights Reserved to the Landlord

1. The right of access to the Property on reasonable notice (not less than 48 hours except in emergency) to inspect, repair, or carry out works to the Building or adjoining property.
2. The right to the passage of utilities through any conduits in, under, or over the Property.
3. The right to erect scaffolding for repair or maintenance of the Building.
4. The right to alter or rebuild any part of the Building not comprised in the Property.

---

## SCHEDULE 3 -- Services and Service Charge

### Services

The Landlord shall provide the following services:

1. Cleaning and lighting of the Common Parts
2. Maintenance of the lifts
3. Upkeep of the exterior and structural parts of the Building
4. Building insurance
5. Security systems and access control
6. Waste disposal
7. Management fees (not exceeding 10% of total service charge expenditure)

### Service Charge

The Tenant's proportion shall be 25% of the total service charge expenditure, subject to the Service Charge Cap.

---

## SCHEDULE 4 -- Rent Review

### 4.1

On the Review Date, the rent shall be reviewed to the higher of:

(a) the rent payable immediately before the Review Date; and

(b) the open market rent of the Property as at the Review Date.

### 4.2

The open market rent shall be determined by agreement between the parties or, failing agreement within 3 months after the Review Date, by an independent surveyor appointed by agreement or, in default of agreement, by the President of the Royal Institution of Chartered Surveyors.

### 4.3 Assumptions

The open market rent shall be assessed on the assumptions that:

(a) the Property is available for letting on the open market by a willing landlord to a willing tenant;

(b) the Property is fit for immediate occupation and use;

(c) the Tenant's covenants have been fully performed; and

(d) no account shall be taken of any goodwill attached to the Property by reason of the Tenant's occupation.
