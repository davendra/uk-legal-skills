## AGREED TERMS

### 1. DEFINITIONS AND INTERPRETATION

#### 1.1 Definitions

In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires:

**"Authorised Persons"** means, in relation to each Party:

(a) its employees, officers, and directors who reasonably need access to the Confidential Information for the Purpose;

(b) its professional advisers (including solicitors, accountants, and auditors) who are bound by professional duties of confidentiality; and

(c) its contractors and consultants who have entered into binding confidentiality obligations no less onerous than those contained in this Agreement;

**"Confidential Information"** means all information (whether written, oral, visual, electronic, or in any other form) disclosed by or on behalf of the Disclosing Party to the Receiving Party (whether before or after the date of this Agreement) that:

(a) is marked or designated as confidential at the time of disclosure; or

(b) would reasonably be considered to be confidential given the nature of the information or the circumstances of its disclosure;

including, without limitation: trade secrets, know-how, inventions, algorithms, source code, software designs, product roadmaps, business plans, financial information, customer and supplier lists, pricing data, marketing strategies, technical specifications, research and development information, and any analyses, compilations, studies, or other documents prepared by the Receiving Party which contain, reflect, or are derived from such information;

**"Disclosing Party"** means the Party disclosing Confidential Information;

**"Effective Date"** means the date of this Agreement;

**"Receiving Party"** means the Party receiving Confidential Information;

**"Term"** has the meaning given in Clause 7.1.

#### 1.2 Interpretation

(a) References to clauses are to clauses of this Agreement.

(b) References to statutes include any statutory modification or re-enactment thereof.

(c) The headings in this Agreement are for convenience only and shall not affect its interpretation.

(d) Words in the singular include the plural and vice versa.

### 2. CONFIDENTIALITY OBLIGATIONS

#### 2.1 Undertakings

Each Receiving Party undertakes to the Disclosing Party that it shall:

(a) keep the Confidential Information strictly confidential;

(b) not disclose the Confidential Information to any person other than its Authorised Persons;

(c) use the Confidential Information only for the Purpose;

(d) not copy or reproduce the Confidential Information except as reasonably necessary for the Purpose;

(e) apply to the Confidential Information no lesser standard of care than it applies to its own confidential information of a similar nature, and in any event no less than a reasonable standard of care;

(f) ensure that each of its Authorised Persons to whom it discloses Confidential Information is made aware of and complies with the obligations of confidentiality under this Agreement; and

(g) be responsible for any breach of this Agreement by its Authorised Persons as if such breach were its own.

#### 2.2 No Licence or Rights

Nothing in this Agreement shall grant the Receiving Party any right, title, or interest in the Confidential Information, nor any licence under any intellectual property rights of the Disclosing Party.

#### 2.3 No Warranty

The Disclosing Party makes no representation or warranty, express or implied, as to the accuracy, completeness, or reliability of the Confidential Information. The Receiving Party shall be solely responsible for its use of and reliance upon the Confidential Information.

---

### 3. PERMITTED DISCLOSURES AND EXCLUSIONS

#### 3.1 Exclusions from Confidential Information

The obligations in Clause 2 shall not apply to information that the Receiving Party can demonstrate by reasonable written evidence:

(a) **Public Domain:** is or becomes generally available to the public other than as a result of a breach of this Agreement by the Receiving Party or its Authorised Persons;

(b) **Prior Knowledge:** was already in the lawful possession of the Receiving Party before disclosure by the Disclosing Party, as evidenced by the Receiving Party's written records existing prior to such disclosure;

(c) **Independent Development:** was independently developed by the Receiving Party without reference to or use of the Confidential Information, as evidenced by contemporaneous written records;

(d) **Third Party Disclosure:** was lawfully received by the Receiving Party from a third party who was not, to the Receiving Party's knowledge, under any obligation of confidentiality in respect of that information; or

(e) **Agreed Release:** was released from the obligations of confidentiality by the prior written consent of the Disclosing Party.

#### 3.2 Required Disclosures

The Receiving Party may disclose Confidential Information to the minimum extent required:

(a) by any applicable law, regulation, or governmental or regulatory order;

(b) by any court of competent jurisdiction or any competent judicial, governmental, supervisory, or regulatory body;

(c) by the rules of any listing authority, stock exchange, or competent regulatory body to which the Receiving Party is subject;

provided that, where legally permitted, the Receiving Party shall:

(i) give the Disclosing Party as much advance written notice as reasonably practicable of such required disclosure;

(ii) consult with the Disclosing Party as to the form, content, and timing of the disclosure;

(iii) take into account the reasonable requests of the Disclosing Party regarding the disclosure; and

(iv) disclose only the minimum amount of Confidential Information necessary to comply with the requirement.

---

### 4. RETURN AND DESTRUCTION OF CONFIDENTIAL INFORMATION

#### 4.1

Upon the earlier of:

(a) the written request of the Disclosing Party at any time; or

(b) the expiry or termination of this Agreement,

the Receiving Party shall promptly (and in any event within 14 days):

(i) return to the Disclosing Party all documents, materials, and other tangible items containing or reflecting any Confidential Information;

(ii) permanently delete or destroy all Confidential Information stored in electronic form (including any copies, extracts, or summaries thereof); and

(iii) provide written certification signed by an authorised officer of the Receiving Party confirming that it has complied with its obligations under this Clause 4.

#### 4.2 Retained Copies

Notwithstanding Clause 4.1, the Receiving Party may retain:

(a) copies of Confidential Information to the extent required by applicable law or regulation or by any competent regulatory authority; and

(b) copies of Confidential Information contained in routine electronic back-up or archival systems, provided that such copies shall remain subject to the confidentiality obligations in this Agreement.

### 5. REMEDIES

#### 5.1 Injunctive Relief

Each Party acknowledges that damages alone may not be an adequate remedy for any breach of this Agreement. Accordingly, the Disclosing Party shall be entitled to seek injunctive relief (including interim and final injunctions) in addition to any other remedy available at law or in equity, without the need to prove actual loss or damage and without the need to post any bond or other security.

#### 5.2 Costs

In the event of any proceedings arising from a breach of this Agreement, the unsuccessful Party shall pay the reasonable legal costs of the successful Party on the **standard basis** as assessed by the court if not agreed between the Parties.

#### 5.3 Cumulative Remedies

The rights and remedies of the Parties under this Agreement are cumulative and not exclusive of any rights or remedies provided by law.

---

### 6. DATA PROTECTION

#### 6.1

To the extent that any Confidential Information includes personal data (as defined in the UK General Data Protection Regulation and the Data Protection Act 2018), each Party shall comply with its obligations as a controller or processor (as applicable) under the UK GDPR and Data Protection Act 2018.

#### 6.2

Neither Party shall transfer any personal data received under this Agreement outside of the United Kingdom without the prior written consent of the Disclosing Party and adequate safeguards in accordance with Articles 46-49 of the UK GDPR.

---

### 7. TERM AND SURVIVAL

#### 7.1 Term

This Agreement shall come into force on the Effective Date and shall continue for a period of **3 (three) years** from the Effective Date, unless terminated earlier by either Party giving not less than 30 days' prior written notice to the other Party (the **"Term"**).

#### 7.2 Survival

The obligations of confidentiality set out in Clause 2 shall survive the expiry or termination of this Agreement for a further period of **2 (two) years** from the date of such expiry or termination.

### 8. GENERAL

#### 8.1 No Partnership or Agency

Nothing in this Agreement shall create, or be deemed to create, a partnership, joint venture, or relationship of employer and employee or principal and agent between the Parties.

#### 8.2 No Obligation to Proceed

Nothing in this Agreement shall oblige either Party to enter into any further agreement, transaction, or arrangement with the other Party, nor shall this Agreement constitute an offer capable of acceptance.

#### 8.3 Entire Agreement

This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the Parties relating to its subject matter and supersedes all previous agreements, understandings, and arrangements between them, whether written or oral.

#### 8.4 Variation

No variation of this Agreement shall be effective unless it is in writing and signed by or on behalf of each Party.

#### 8.5 Waiver

No failure or delay by a Party to exercise any right or remedy provided under this Agreement or by law shall constitute a waiver of that or any other right or remedy, nor shall it prevent or restrict the further exercise of that or any other right or remedy.

#### 8.6 Assignment

Neither Party may assign, transfer, charge, or deal in any other manner with all or any of its rights or obligations under this Agreement without the prior written consent of the other Party.

#### 8.7 Notices

Any notice under this Agreement shall be in writing and shall be delivered by hand, sent by pre-paid first class post, or sent by email to the addresses set out above (or to such other address as a Party may notify in writing). A notice shall be deemed received:

(a) if delivered by hand, on the date of delivery;

(b) if sent by pre-paid first class post, on the second Business Day after posting;

(c) if sent by email, on the next Business Day after sending (provided no delivery failure notification is received).

#### 8.8 Severability

If any provision of this Agreement is held to be invalid, illegal, or unenforceable by any court or competent authority, it shall be deemed modified to the minimum extent necessary to make it valid, legal, and enforceable. If such modification is not possible, the provision shall be deemed severed without affecting the validity of the remaining provisions.

#### 8.9 Third Party Rights

A person who is not a party to this Agreement shall not have any rights under the Contracts (Rights of Third Parties) Act 1999 to enforce any term of this Agreement.

#### 8.10 Governing Law

This Agreement and any dispute or claim (including non-contractual disputes or claims) arising out of or in connection with it or its subject matter or formation shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the law of **England and Wales**.

#### 8.11 Jurisdiction

Each Party irrevocably agrees that the courts of **England and Wales** shall have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any dispute or claim (including non-contractual disputes or claims) arising out of or in connection with this Agreement or its subject matter or formation.
