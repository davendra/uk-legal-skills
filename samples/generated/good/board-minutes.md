## 6. FINANCE DIRECTOR'S REPORT

**6.1** Priya Kapoor presented the Finance Director's report for the financial year ended 31 December 2025. Key points included:

- **Revenue:** GBP 8.7 million (2024: GBP 7.9 million), representing 10.1% year-on-year growth
- **Gross profit margin:** 34.2% (2024: 32.8%)
- **EBITDA:** GBP 1.42 million (2024: GBP 1.18 million)
- **Net profit after tax:** GBP 892,000 (2024: GBP 714,000)
- **Cash and cash equivalents:** GBP 1.21 million as at 31 December 2025
- **Net assets:** GBP 3.45 million (2024: GBP 2.89 million)
- **Debtor days:** 42 (2024: 47) -- improvement following tighter credit control
- **Product liability insurance:** Renewed with Hiscox at annual premium of GBP 18,500 (2024: GBP 16,200), reflecting increased turnover

**6.2** The Board discussed the financial results. Robert Ashworth noted the strong performance in the automotive components division, which had grown revenue by 18% following the new contract with Jaguar Land Rover.

**6.3** The Board noted the Finance Director's report.

---

## 7. MANAGING DIRECTOR'S REPORT

**7.1** Robert Ashworth presented the Managing Director's report, covering:

- **Operations:** Production capacity utilisation at 87% (target 85%). No significant health and safety incidents in the period. ISO 9001:2015 certification maintained following the surveillance audit in October 2025.
- **Workforce:** Total headcount of 124 employees (2024: 118). Apprenticeship programme expanded to 8 apprentices. Staff turnover at 11% (2024: 14%).
- **ESG:** Progress on carbon reduction targets -- Scope 1 and 2 emissions reduced by 7% year-on-year. Solar panel installation on warehouse roof completed in September 2025.
- **Regulatory:** Compliance review of the Building Safety Act 2022 implications for manufacturing premises underway. Employment contract review in progress to ensure compliance with Employment Rights Act 2025 amendments.

**7.2** The Board noted the Managing Director's report.

## 8. RESOLUTIONS

### Resolution 1: Approval of Annual Accounts

**8.1** The Board considered the annual accounts for the financial year ended 31 December 2025, which had been prepared in accordance with Financial Reporting Standard 102 (FRS 102) and the Companies Act 2006.

**8.2** The Finance Director confirmed that the accounts gave a true and fair view of the Company's financial position and had been reviewed by the external auditor.

**8.3** It was **RESOLVED** (unanimously, all 4 directors voting in favour):

> **THAT** the annual accounts of Oakwood Manufacturing Limited for the financial year ended 31 December 2025, as presented to the Board, be and are hereby approved for the purposes of section 414 of the Companies Act 2006, and that the Chair be authorised to sign the balance sheet and directors' report on behalf of the Board pursuant to sections 414 and 419 of the Companies Act 2006.

### Resolution 2: Declaration of Final Dividend

**8.4** The Board considered the recommendation of the Finance Director to declare a final dividend for the financial year ended 31 December 2025.

**8.5** The Finance Director confirmed that:

(a) The Company had sufficient distributable profits to pay the proposed dividend, in accordance with Part 23 of the Companies Act 2006 (sections 829-853);

(b) The relevant accounts for the purposes of section 836 of the Companies Act 2006 were the annual accounts for the year ended 31 December 2025;

(c) The proposed dividend would not reduce the Company's net assets below the aggregate of its called-up share capital and undistributable reserves; and

(d) The Company would remain solvent after payment of the dividend.

**8.6** It was **RESOLVED** (unanimously, all 4 directors voting in favour):

> **THAT** a final dividend of **GBP 0.45 per ordinary share** (totalling GBP 225,000 based on 500,000 issued ordinary shares) be and is hereby declared in respect of the financial year ended 31 December 2025, payable on **15 April 2026** to shareholders on the register at the close of business on **31 March 2026** (the **"Record Date"**), and that the Company Secretary be instructed to arrange payment accordingly.

### Resolution 3: Appointment of Auditor

**8.7** The Board considered the appointment of the Company's auditor for the financial year ending 31 December 2026.

**8.8** It was noted that Simmons & Partners LLP had served as the Company's auditor for the previous 3 financial years and had provided satisfactory service. The Finance Director confirmed that the proposed audit fee for the year ending 31 December 2026 was GBP 22,500 (2025: GBP 21,000), which was considered reasonable for the scope of work.

**8.9** James Whitfield confirmed his abstention from voting on this resolution, as declared under item 5.2 above.

**8.10** It was **RESOLVED** (3 directors voting in favour, 1 abstention (James Whitfield)):

> **THAT** Simmons & Partners LLP, Chartered Accountants, of 40 Temple Row, Birmingham B2 5LS, be and are hereby appointed as auditor of the Company for the financial year ending 31 December 2026, pursuant to section 485 of the Companies Act 2006, and that the Board be authorised to fix their remuneration pursuant to section 492(1) of the Companies Act 2006.
