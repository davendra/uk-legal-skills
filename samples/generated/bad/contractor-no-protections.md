## BACKGROUND

## 1. ENGAGEMENT

1.1 The Company hereby engages the Contractor to perform such services as the Company may from time to time require ("the Services"). The scope, nature, and extent of the Services shall be determined by the Company in its sole and absolute discretion.

1.2 The Contractor shall perform the Services at such times, at such locations, and in such manner as the Company shall direct. The Contractor shall be available to perform the Services on any day and at any time as required by the Company, including weekends and public holidays.

1.3 The Contractor shall commence the Services on the date of this Agreement and shall continue to provide the Services indefinitely, unless this Agreement is terminated in accordance with its terms.

---

## 2. FEES AND PAYMENT

2.1 The Company shall pay the Contractor a fee for the Services at a rate to be agreed between the parties from time to time.

2.2 **No specific payment terms, payment schedule, invoicing procedure, payment method, or currency of payment are specified in this Agreement.** The Company shall pay the Contractor at such times and in such manner as the Company deems appropriate.

2.3 The Company may set off against any fees due to the Contractor any amounts owed by the Contractor to the Company under this Agreement or otherwise, without prior notice and without limitation.

2.4 The Contractor shall be solely responsible for all taxes, national insurance contributions, VAT, and any other fiscal obligations arising from the fees paid under this Agreement. The Company shall not be required to make any deductions or withholdings from the fees.

---

## 3. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

3.1 **All intellectual property rights** in any work, materials, documents, software, inventions, designs, databases, reports, or other output created, developed, conceived, or contributed to by the Contractor at any time during the term of this Agreement ("the Works") shall vest in and be the absolute property of the Company.

3.2 For the avoidance of doubt, **the Works include:**

   (a) all work performed by the Contractor for the Company under this Agreement;

   (b) all work performed by the Contractor outside the scope of this Agreement but during the term of this Agreement, including personal projects, open-source contributions, and work performed for third parties;

   (c) **all intellectual property, inventions, designs, software, and works created by the Contractor prior to the commencement of this Agreement** ("Prior Inventions"), which the Contractor hereby irrevocably assigns to the Company absolutely and unconditionally;

   (d) all modifications, improvements, derivative works, and enhancements to any of the foregoing;

   (e) all moral rights in the Works, which the Contractor hereby irrevocably waives to the fullest extent permitted by law.

3.3 The Contractor shall execute all documents and do all things necessary to give effect to the assignments and waivers in this clause 3, and hereby irrevocably appoints the Company as the Contractor's attorney for the purpose of executing any such documents.

3.4 The Contractor represents and warrants that no Prior Inventions exist other than those listed in Schedule 1 to this Agreement. **[NOTE: No Schedule 1 is attached to this Agreement.]**

## 4. LIABILITY AND INDEMNITY

4.1 **The Contractor shall be personally liable** for any and all losses, damages, costs, claims, demands, actions, proceedings, expenses (including legal expenses on a full indemnity basis), fines, penalties, and liabilities of any nature whatsoever suffered or incurred by the Company, its group companies, its clients, or any third party, arising out of or in connection with:

   (a) the performance or non-performance of the Services;

   (b) any act or omission of the Contractor;

   (c) any breach of this Agreement by the Contractor;

   (d) any claim by any third party in connection with the Services or the Works;

   (e) any matter arising out of or in connection with this Agreement, howsoever arising, including any claim in negligence, breach of statutory duty, or otherwise.

4.2 **The Contractor's liability under this Agreement is unlimited.** No cap, ceiling, or limitation of any kind shall apply to the Contractor's liability, whether in contract, tort (including negligence), breach of statutory duty, or otherwise.

4.3 **The Contractor hereby agrees that the Company may pursue claims directly against the Contractor personally, notwithstanding that the Contractor may operate through a limited company or other corporate vehicle.** The Contractor acknowledges that the protections of limited liability shall not apply to the Contractor's obligations under this Agreement, and the Contractor's personal assets (including residential property, savings, investments, and pension) shall be available to satisfy any claim by the Company.

4.4 The Contractor shall indemnify and keep indemnified the Company and its directors, officers, employees, and agents against **any and all claims, losses, damages, costs, and expenses without limitation** arising directly or indirectly out of or in connection with this Agreement, the Services, or the Contractor's acts or omissions.

4.5 **The Company's liability to the Contractor under this Agreement shall not exceed the total fees paid to the Contractor in the twelve (12) months preceding the date of any claim, or GBP 1,000, whichever is less.**

## 5. CONFIDENTIALITY

5.1 The Contractor shall keep confidential all information relating to the Company, its business, clients, suppliers, and affairs, and shall not disclose such information to any third party without the prior written consent of the Company.

5.2 The obligations of confidentiality shall survive the termination of this Agreement indefinitely.

---

## 6. TERMINATION

6.1 **This Agreement does not contain any express termination provisions.** Neither party has the right to terminate this Agreement by notice or otherwise, except:

   (a) the Company may terminate this Agreement immediately and without notice if the Contractor breaches any provision of this Agreement (as determined by the Company in its sole discretion);

   (b) the Company may terminate this Agreement immediately and without notice if the Company, in its sole discretion, determines that the Contractor's services are no longer required.

6.2 The Contractor has no right to terminate this Agreement. The Contractor acknowledges that any purported termination by the Contractor shall constitute a material breach of this Agreement, entitling the Company to claim damages (including consequential and indirect losses) without limitation.

6.3 Upon termination of this Agreement (however arising), the Contractor shall:

   (a) immediately cease performing the Services;

   (b) deliver to the Company all Works, documents, and materials in the Contractor's possession or control;

   (c) return all Company property;

   (d) continue to be bound by clauses 3, 4, and 5 of this Agreement.

## 7. STATUS

7.1 The Contractor is engaged as an independent contractor and nothing in this Agreement shall create a relationship of employer and employee, partnership, joint venture, or agency between the parties.

7.2 The Contractor shall not be entitled to any employment rights, benefits, or protections, including (without limitation) holiday pay, sick pay, pension contributions, redundancy pay, or unfair dismissal protection.

---

## 8. RESTRICTIVE COVENANTS

8.1 For a period of twelve (12) months following the termination of this Agreement, the Contractor shall not:

   (b) solicit or entice away any employee or contractor of the Company;

   (c) engage in any business that competes with the Company.
