## 1. INTRODUCTION

Quick Conveyancing Ltd (Company No. 12493018) is a conveyancing firm based at 14 Station Road, Slough SL1 1QY. We provide residential and commercial conveyancing services to clients across England and Wales.

This policy sets out our procedures for complying with anti-money laundering legislation, including the Proceeds of Crime Act 2002, the Terrorism Act 2000, and the Money Laundering, Terrorist Financing and Transfer of Funds (Information on the Payer) Regulations 2017 (as amended).

We are regulated by the Council for Licensed Conveyancers (CLC).

---

## 2. MONEY LAUNDERING REPORTING OFFICER (MLRO)

An MLRO has not yet been appointed. The Office Manager, Derek Simmons, will handle any queries relating to anti-money laundering compliance on an ad hoc basis until a formal appointment is made.

No deputy MLRO has been appointed.

No nominated officer has been appointed for the purposes of Part 7 of the Proceeds of Crime Act 2002 or Part 3 of the Terrorism Act 2000.

---

## 3. CLIENT DUE DILIGENCE (CDD)

### 3.1 Standard Client Due Diligence

When taking on a new client, we verify the client's identity by requesting one of the following documents:

- A valid passport; **OR**
- A UK driving licence (photocard)

We take a photocopy of the document provided and place it on the client file.

No further verification is required.

### 3.2 Verification of Address

We ask the client to confirm their address verbally. No documentary evidence of address is required.

### 3.3 Beneficial Ownership

Where the client is a company, we ask the client to confirm the names of the directors. We do not carry out Companies House searches, verify persons of significant control (PSCs), or establish beneficial ownership structures.

Where the client is a trust, we accept the instructions of the named trustee without further enquiry as to the identity of the settlor, beneficiaries, or protector.

### 3.4 Source of Funds

We do not routinely enquire into the source of funds for a transaction. If a client volunteers information about their source of funds, we note it on the file but do not verify it.

### 3.5 Source of Wealth

We do not enquire into the source of wealth of any client.

---

## 4. ENHANCED DUE DILIGENCE (EDD)

Enhanced due diligence is not required for any client or transaction, regardless of risk factors.

We do not apply enhanced due diligence in any of the following circumstances:

- Politically Exposed Persons (PEPs) or their family members and known close associates
- Clients based in or connected to high-risk third countries identified by the Financial Action Task Force (FATF)
- Complex or unusually large transactions
- Transactions with no apparent economic or lawful purpose
- Correspondent relationships
- Non-face-to-face business relationships
- Any other situation where there is a higher risk of money laundering or terrorist financing

If a client is identified as a PEP, we treat them the same as any other client.

---

## 5. ONGOING MONITORING

We do not conduct ongoing monitoring of business relationships or transactions.

Once client due diligence has been completed at the start of the retainer, no further checks or reviews are carried out, regardless of the duration of the business relationship or the number of transactions undertaken.

We do not review or update CDD records at any point.

---

## 6. SANCTIONS SCREENING

We do not screen clients, beneficial owners, or connected parties against:

- HM Treasury's Office of Financial Sanctions Implementation (OFSI) consolidated list
- The United Nations Security Council sanctions lists
- The European Union sanctions lists
- The Office of Foreign Assets Control (OFAC) Specially Designated Nationals list
- Any other sanctions list or database

We rely on the client's self-declaration that they are not subject to financial sanctions. No independent verification is undertaken.

---

## 7. SUSPICIOUS ACTIVITY REPORTS (SARs)

If a member of staff suspects that a transaction may involve money laundering or terrorist financing, they should raise the matter informally with the Office Manager.

No formal SAR procedure has been documented. No internal SAR form has been created.

There is no procedure for submitting SARs to the National Crime Agency (NCA).

There is no procedure for requesting a Defence Against Money Laundering (DAML) consent from the NCA.

There is no procedure for managing the tipping-off risk under sections 333A-333E of the Proceeds of Crime Act 2002.

Staff should use their judgement when deciding whether to proceed with a transaction where suspicion has been identified. In the absence of an MLRO, staff may proceed with a transaction at their own discretion.

---

## 8. FIRM-WIDE RISK ASSESSMENT

A firm-wide risk assessment has not been completed.

We have not assessed the money laundering and terrorist financing risks associated with:

- Our client base
- The types of transactions we undertake
- The geographic areas in which our clients and their transactions are based
- Our delivery channels
- The products and services we offer
- New or developing technologies

We do not categorise clients or transactions by risk level (low, medium, or high).

---

## 9. TRAINING

All staff are required to read this policy once per year.

No formal AML training programme is in place. No training records are maintained.

No training is provided on:

- How to identify suspicious transactions
- How to submit a SAR
- Red flags for money laundering in conveyancing
- Tipping-off obligations
- The legal consequences of non-compliance
- The Proceeds of Crime Act 2002 or the Money Laundering Regulations 2017

New staff are given a copy of this policy on their first day. No induction training on AML is provided.

---

## 10. RECORD RETENTION

No record retention policy is in place.

Client files and CDD records are retained for as long as storage space permits. Files may be destroyed at any time at the discretion of the Office Manager.

We do not maintain a record of:

- SARs submitted to the NCA
- CDD measures applied to individual clients
- The results of ongoing monitoring
- Training provided to staff
- Risk assessments

---

## 11. RELIANCE AND OUTSOURCING

We may accept CDD carried out by other professionals (such as estate agents or mortgage brokers) without verifying the adequacy of their procedures or obtaining copies of the CDD documentation.

We do not enter into formal reliance agreements as contemplated by Regulation 39 of the Money Laundering Regulations 2017.
