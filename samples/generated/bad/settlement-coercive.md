# SETTLEMENT AGREEMENT

## (Without Prejudice and Subject to Contract)

**Date:** 7 March 2026

---

## PARTIES

1. **AggressiveCorp Ltd** (Company No. 09281456), a company incorporated in England and Wales whose registered office is at Apex Tower, 15 Marshwall, London E14 9SH ("the Employer")

2. **Sarah Nkemdirim** of 88 Oaktree Avenue, Croydon CR0 6PL ("the Employee")

---

## BACKGROUND

(A) The Employee has been employed by the Employer as a Senior Marketing Manager since 14 September 2020 at a current annual salary of GBP 55,000.

(B) The Employer has informed the Employee that her employment is to be terminated with immediate effect.

(C) The Employer is prepared to offer the Employee the terms set out in this Agreement in full and final settlement of all and any claims arising out of or connected with her employment or its termination.

(D) **This offer is open for acceptance until 5:00 PM on 9 March 2026 (48 hours from the date of this Agreement). If not accepted by that time, this offer shall lapse automatically and without further notice, and the Employer shall proceed with termination without any settlement payment.**

---

## 1. TERMINATION

1.1 The Employee's employment with the Employer shall terminate on the date of this Agreement ("the Termination Date").

1.2 The Employee shall not be entitled to any notice or payment in lieu of notice, the Employee having agreed to waive her right to notice by entering into this Agreement.

1.3 The Employee shall not be entitled to any accrued but untaken holiday pay, bonus, commission, or any other payment in respect of her employment, save as expressly provided in this Agreement.

---

## 2. SETTLEMENT PAYMENT

2.1 Subject to the Employee complying with all the terms of this Agreement, the Employer shall pay the Employee the sum of **GBP 500 (Five Hundred Pounds)** ("the Settlement Payment") within 28 days of the date of this Agreement.

2.2 The Settlement Payment shall be paid subject to deductions for income tax and National Insurance contributions as the Employer considers appropriate.

2.3 The Settlement Payment is offered in full and final settlement of all and any claims the Employee has or may have against the Employer, whether known or unknown, whether arising under contract, statute, common law, or otherwise (as set out more fully in clause 4 below).

2.4 In the event that the Employee breaches any term of this Agreement, the Employer shall be entitled to recover the Settlement Payment in full, together with interest at 8% per annum from the date of payment, and the Employee shall immediately repay the same on demand.

---

## 3. INDEPENDENT LEGAL ADVICE

3.1 The Employee confirms that she has had the opportunity to take independent legal advice on the terms and effect of this Agreement.

3.2 **The Employer is not obliged to pay for or contribute towards the cost of any legal advice obtained by the Employee.** The Employee shall bear the full cost of any legal advice she chooses to obtain.

3.3 **[NOTE: No independent legal adviser has been identified in this Agreement. No certificate of advice from a relevant independent adviser is attached as required by section 203(3) of the Employment Rights Act 1996.]**

---

## 4. WAIVER OF CLAIMS

4.1 The Employee agrees that the terms of this Agreement are in full and final settlement of **all and any claims, rights of action, complaints, or proceedings** of whatsoever nature, whether contractual, statutory, tortious, or otherwise, which the Employee has or may have against the Employer, its group companies, directors, officers, employees, or agents, arising out of or in connection with:

   (a) the Employee's employment with the Employer;

   (b) the termination of the Employee's employment;

   (c) any act or omission of the Employer during the course of the Employee's employment;

   (d) **any matter whatsoever, whether or not connected with the Employee's employment, whether known to the Employee at the date of this Agreement or not, and whether arising before, on, or after the date of this Agreement.**

4.2 Without limitation, the claims settled by this Agreement include (whether or not they have been raised or are in contemplation):

   (a) unfair dismissal under the Employment Rights Act 1996;

   (b) wrongful dismissal;

   (c) discrimination on grounds of sex, race, disability, age, religion or belief, sexual orientation, gender reassignment, marriage and civil partnership, or pregnancy and maternity under the Equality Act 2010;

   (d) harassment or victimisation under the Equality Act 2010;

   (e) equal pay under the Equality Act 2010;

   (f) unlawful deduction from wages under Part II of the Employment Rights Act 1996;

   (g) breach of the Working Time Regulations 1998;

   (h) failure to provide a written statement of particulars under section 1 of the Employment Rights Act 1996;

   (i) claims under the Transfer of Undertakings (Protection of Employment) Regulations 2006;

   (j) claims under the Part-Time Workers (Prevention of Less Favourable Treatment) Regulations 2000;

   (k) claims under the Fixed-Term Employees (Prevention of Less Favourable Treatment) Regulations 2002;

   (l) claims under the National Minimum Wage Act 1998;

   (m) whistleblowing claims under Part IVA of the Employment Rights Act 1996;

   (n) **claims for personal injury** (whether physical or psychiatric, and whether known or unknown at the date of this Agreement);

   (o) **claims for accrued pension rights;**

   (p) claims under the Human Rights Act 1998;

   (q) any other claims whatsoever and howsoever arising.

4.3 The Employee acknowledges that the claims listed in clause 4.2 are the particular complaints and proceedings which the parties have agreed to settle, as required by section 203(3)(ab) of the Employment Rights Act 1996 and regulation 35(3)(b) of the Equality Act 2010.

---

## 5. NON-DISPARAGEMENT

5.1 The Employee shall not at any time (whether during or after the termination of her employment) make, publish, or cause to be published any disparaging, derogatory, negative, critical, or unfavourable statement, comment, or remark about the Employer, its business, products, services, directors, officers, employees, or agents to any person.

5.2 **The obligations in clause 5.1 extend to all forms of communication, including but not limited to:**

   (a) verbal statements to any person;

   (b) written communications, including letters, emails, and text messages;

   (c) social media posts, comments, reviews, or messages on any platform, including but not limited to LinkedIn, Twitter/X, Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, Glassdoor, Indeed, Trustpilot, Reddit, and any other current or future social media platform;

   (d) reviews on employer review websites;

   (e) posts in online forums, discussion groups, or communities;

   (f) communications with journalists, bloggers, influencers, or media organisations;

   (g) communications with the Employer's clients, customers, suppliers, or business contacts.

5.3 The obligations in clause 5.1 shall continue **in perpetuity** and shall survive the termination of this Agreement.

5.4 The Employee shall not disclose the existence or terms of this Agreement to any person other than her immediate family and professional advisers, who shall also be bound by this restriction.

5.5 **The Employer makes no reciprocal commitment regarding non-disparagement.**

---

## 6. CLAWBACK — COMPETITOR RESTRICTION

6.1 **If the Employee at any time within twenty-four (24) months of the Termination Date accepts employment, an appointment, an engagement, or provides services (whether as employee, consultant, director, partner, or otherwise) with any entity that the Employer considers to be a competitor** (as determined by the Employer in its sole and absolute discretion), the Employee shall:

   (a) immediately repay the Settlement Payment in full;

   (b) pay to the Employer liquidated damages of GBP 10,000;

   (c) be released from the benefit of clause 4 (waiver of claims), such that the Employer may bring any claims against the Employee that would otherwise have been settled.

---

## 7. REFERENCES

7.1 The Employer shall provide a reference for the Employee in the form set out in Schedule 1 to this Agreement.

7.2 **The reference in Schedule 1 shall only be provided on the condition that the Employee continues to comply with all the terms of this Agreement, including the non-disparagement obligations in clause 5 and the competitor restriction in clause 6. If the Employee breaches any term of this Agreement, the Employer shall be under no obligation to provide any reference and may withdraw any reference previously given.**

7.3 The Employer reserves the right to disclose to any prospective employer that the Employee's employment was terminated and that a settlement agreement was entered into, if the Employer in its discretion considers it appropriate to do so.

---

## 8. RETURN OF PROPERTY

8.1 The Employee shall return all property belonging to the Employer within 24 hours of the Termination Date.

8.2 Failure to return any item shall entitle the Employer to deduct the replacement cost from the Settlement Payment.

---

## 9. GOVERNING LAW

9.1 This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with English law.

9.2 The parties submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the English courts.
