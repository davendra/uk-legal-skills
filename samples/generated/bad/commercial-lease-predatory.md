# LEASE OF COMMERCIAL PREMISES

## Unit 14B, Irongate Yard, Bermondsey Street, London SE1 3XF

**Date:** 15 March 2026

2. **TENANT:** Amira Okonkwo trading as "Botanical & Bloom" of 42 Rye Lane, London SE15 5BS ("the Tenant")

3. **GUARANTOR:** Amira Okonkwo personally of 42 Rye Lane, London SE15 5BS ("the Guarantor")

## 1. DEFINITIONS AND INTERPRETATION

1.1 **"Premises"** means the ground floor retail unit known as Unit 14B, Irongate Yard, Bermondsey Street, London SE1 3XF, including all fixtures, fittings, walls (both structural and non-structural), roof, foundations, drains, pipes, conduits, wiring, and all mechanical and electrical systems serving the Premises, as shown edged red on the plan attached at Schedule 1.

1.2 **"Building"** means the entirety of Irongate Yard, a Grade II listed building dating from approximately 1876, comprising 22 commercial units across four floors.

1.3 **"Term"** means a period of twenty-five (25) years commencing on 1 April 2026 and expiring on 31 March 2051.

1.4 **"Initial Rent"** means GBP 48,000 (Forty-Eight Thousand Pounds) per annum exclusive of VAT, payable quarterly in advance on the usual quarter days.

1.5 **"Permitted Use"** means retail use within Use Class E only, subject to the Landlord's prior written consent for any change of specific retail activity, such consent to be given or withheld at the Landlord's absolute and unfettered discretion.

---

## 2. GRANT

2.1 The Landlord demises the Premises to the Tenant for the Term at the Initial Rent subject to annual review as set out in clause 5, SUBJECT TO AND WITH THE BENEFIT OF the matters contained or referred to in the property register and charges register of Title Number TGL483921.

2.2 This lease is granted WITHOUT security of tenure. The provisions of sections 24 to 28 of the Landlord and Tenant Act 1954 are excluded in relation to the tenancy created by this lease.

**[NOTE: No contracting-out procedure under section 38A of the Landlord and Tenant Act 1954 has been followed. No health warning notice has been served on the Tenant. No declaration (ordinary or statutory) has been made by the Tenant.]**

---

## 3. TENANT'S COVENANTS

### 3.1 Repair — Full Repairing and Insuring Obligation

3.1.1 The Tenant shall keep the Premises including all structural and non-structural parts, the roof, foundations, external walls, load-bearing walls, drains, gutters, downpipes, all mechanical plant, electrical installations, and all conduits exclusively serving or passing through the Premises in good and substantial repair and condition throughout the Term.

3.1.2 The Tenant shall be responsible for any works required by any statutory authority, including but not limited to works required under the Building Act 1984, the Housing Act 2004, fire safety legislation, and any requirements arising from the listed building status of the Building, regardless of the cost of such works and regardless of the age or inherent condition of the Building.

3.1.3 At the expiration or sooner determination of the Term, the Tenant shall yield up the Premises in a state of full repair and decoration consistent with the obligations contained in this lease. For the avoidance of doubt, the Tenant's obligation extends to remedying any inherent or latent defects in the Building, regardless of whether such defects existed prior to the commencement of the Term.

3.1.4 No schedule of condition has been prepared or annexed to this lease.

### 3.2 Decoration

3.2.1 The Tenant shall decorate the interior of the Premises in the last year of the Term and at intervals of not more than three (3) years during the Term, using materials and colours approved in advance by the Landlord, and shall decorate the exterior in every other year (subject to listed building consent where applicable, which the Tenant shall obtain and bear the full cost of).

### 3.3 Alterations

3.3.1 The Tenant shall not make any alteration or addition whatsoever to the Premises, whether structural or non-structural, internal or external, without the Landlord's prior written consent, which may be withheld for any reason or no reason at all.

3.3.2 If consent is granted, the Tenant shall pay the Landlord's legal and surveyor's fees in connection with any application for consent, whether or not consent is ultimately granted.

### 3.4 Insurance

3.4.1 The Landlord shall insure the Building and the Tenant shall reimburse the Landlord for the full cost of such insurance, including any loading on the premium arising from the Tenant's use of the Premises, the age or condition of the Building, or any other factor.

3.4.2 The Tenant shall be responsible for and shall indemnify the Landlord against any uninsured risks and any shortfall in insurance recovery, including any excess or deductible.

---

## 4. NO BREAK CLAUSE

4.1 This lease does not contain any break clause. The Tenant has no right to terminate this lease prior to the expiry of the Term on 31 March 2051.

4.2 In the event the Tenant wishes to vacate the Premises before the end of the Term, the Tenant shall remain liable for all rent, service charge, insurance contributions, and all other sums due under this lease until the earlier of (a) the expiry of the Term or (b) the date upon which the Landlord (in its absolute discretion) accepts a surrender of the lease, and the Tenant shall pay a surrender premium equivalent to two (2) years' rent at the then current rate as a condition of any such surrender.

---

## 5. RENT REVIEW

5.1 The rent payable under this lease shall be reviewed on every anniversary of the Term Commencement Date ("Review Date").

5.2 On each Review Date the rent shall be adjusted to the higher of:

   (a) the rent payable immediately before the Review Date; and

   (b) the open market rental value of the Premises as at the Review Date, to be determined as if the Premises were available to let on the open market by a willing landlord to a willing tenant.

5.3 **The rent shall only be reviewed in an upward direction.** Under no circumstances shall the revised rent be less than the rent payable immediately before the relevant Review Date, regardless of market conditions, economic downturn, pandemic, or any other factor.

5.4 If the parties cannot agree the revised rent within twenty-eight (28) days of the Review Date, the revised rent shall be determined by a surveyor appointed by the Landlord (at the Tenant's expense). The Landlord's surveyor's determination shall be final and binding on both parties.

5.5 If the revised rent has not been determined by the Review Date, the Tenant shall pay rent at a rate 110% of the current rent on account until such time as the review is concluded, with any overpayment to be credited against future rent (but not refunded).

---

## 6. SERVICE CHARGE

6.1 The Tenant shall pay a fair and reasonable proportion of the costs and expenses incurred by the Landlord in connection with the repair, maintenance, management, and renewal of the Building and any common parts. The Landlord shall determine the Tenant's proportion in its absolute discretion.

6.2 The Landlord's certificate of the service charge shall be conclusive and binding on the Tenant, and the Tenant shall have no right to challenge or query any item of expenditure.

6.3 There shall be no cap on the service charge, and the Landlord reserves the right to undertake works of improvement (as distinct from repair) and to recover the cost from the Tenant as part of the service charge.

---

## 7. LANDLORD'S COSTS

7.1 The Tenant shall pay to the Landlord on demand all costs, fees, charges, disbursements, and expenses (including but not limited to solicitors' fees on a full indemnity basis, surveyors' fees, and management fees) incurred by the Landlord in connection with or arising out of:

   (a) the preparation and grant of this lease;

   (b) any application for consent under this lease, whether or not consent is granted;

   (c) the enforcement or contemplated enforcement of any covenant or obligation contained in this lease;

   (d) the preparation and service of any notice under section 146 of the Law of Property Act 1925, whether or not forfeiture is avoided;

   (e) the collection or recovery of rent or any other sums due under this lease;

   (f) any rent review under this lease;

   (g) any dispute between the Landlord and the Tenant, regardless of outcome;

   (h) any renewal, assignment, subletting, or surrender of this lease;

   (i) the Landlord's management of the Building generally.

---

## 8. ASSIGNMENT AND SUBLETTING

8.1 The Tenant shall not assign, sublet, charge, part with possession of, or share occupation of the whole or any part of the Premises without the Landlord's prior written consent, such consent not to be unreasonably withheld but subject to such conditions as the Landlord may in its absolute discretion impose, including (without limitation):

   (a) that any assignee provides a rent deposit equivalent to twelve (12) months' rent;

   (b) that the Tenant enters into an authorised guarantee agreement guaranteeing the performance of the assignee's obligations for the remainder of the Term;

   (c) that the assignee provides a personal guarantee from a director or individual acceptable to the Landlord;

   (d) that the Tenant pays all of the Landlord's costs in connection with the assignment.

---

## 9. PERSONAL GUARANTEE

9.1 The Guarantor hereby covenants with the Landlord as primary obligor and not merely as surety that the Tenant will pay the rent and observe and perform all the covenants on the Tenant's part contained in this lease.

9.2 The Guarantor's liability under this clause shall be unlimited in amount and shall continue for the entirety of the Term, including any statutory continuation, and shall not be released or diminished by:

   (a) any variation of the terms of this lease;

   (b) any time or indulgence granted by the Landlord to the Tenant;

   (c) the insolvency, winding up, or dissolution of the Tenant;

   (d) any other act, omission, or circumstance which but for this provision might release the Guarantor.

9.3 If this lease is disclaimed in the Tenant's insolvency, the Guarantor shall, at the Landlord's option, accept a new lease of the Premises for a term equal to the residue of the Term at the then current rent, on the same terms as this lease.

---

## 10. FORFEITURE

10.1 The Landlord may re-enter the Premises and forfeit this lease if:

   (a) any rent or other sum due under this lease is in arrears for twenty-one (21) days (whether formally demanded or not);

   (b) the Tenant breaches any covenant contained in this lease;

   (c) the Tenant becomes insolvent, enters administration, or has a receiver appointed.

---

## 11. GOVERNING LAW

11.1 This lease shall be governed by and construed in accordance with English law.

11.2 The parties submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the English courts.
