## 1. DEFINITIONS

1.1 **"Confidential Information"** means any and all information of any nature whatsoever, in any form or medium, whether written, oral, visual, electronic, or otherwise, that has been or may hereafter be disclosed, communicated, or made available by the Discloser to the Recipient, directly or indirectly, whether before or after the date of this Agreement, and regardless of whether such information is marked as confidential or not. Without limitation, Confidential Information includes:

   (a) all business plans, strategies, forecasts, projections, analyses, and financial information;

   (b) all client lists, customer data, supplier information, pricing structures, and commercial terms;

   (c) all intellectual property, trade secrets, know-how, inventions, designs, algorithms, software, source code, and technical information;

   (d) all information relating to directors, officers, employees, consultants, and agents of the Discloser;

   (e) all information relating to any past, current, or future transactions, investments, or business relationships;

   (f) all information disclosed in the course of any meeting, telephone call, email, or other communication;

   (g) the existence and terms of this Agreement;

   (h) the fact that discussions or negotiations are taking place between the Parties;

   (i) all notes, analyses, compilations, studies, interpretations, memoranda, or other documents prepared by the Recipient which contain, reflect, or are based upon, in whole or in part, any of the foregoing;

   (j) any information which a reasonable person would consider confidential in the circumstances, and any information which the Discloser asserts is confidential, whether or not such assertion is reasonable.

1.2 **"Purpose"** means evaluating a potential business relationship between the Parties, the precise nature of which shall be determined by the Discloser.

## 2. OBLIGATIONS OF CONFIDENTIALITY

2.1 The Recipient shall:

   (a) keep all Confidential Information strictly confidential;

   (b) not disclose any Confidential Information to any third party without the prior written consent of the Discloser, such consent to be given or withheld at the Discloser's absolute discretion;

   (c) not use any Confidential Information for any purpose other than the Purpose;

   (d) not copy, reproduce, or reduce to writing any Confidential Information except as strictly necessary for the Purpose and with the Discloser's prior written approval;

   (e) apply to the Confidential Information no lesser security measures and degree of care than those which the Recipient applies to its own most highly confidential information, and in any event no less than reasonable care;

   (f) immediately notify the Discloser of any suspected or actual unauthorised disclosure, use, or loss of Confidential Information.

2.2 The Recipient shall ensure that all of its directors, officers, employees, agents, advisers, and representatives who have access to Confidential Information are bound by obligations of confidentiality no less onerous than those set out in this Agreement.

## 3. NO EXCEPTIONS

3.1 **The obligations of confidentiality in clause 2 shall apply to all Confidential Information without exception.** For the avoidance of doubt, there are no carve-outs or exceptions for information that:

   (a) is or becomes publicly available (whether through no fault of the Recipient or otherwise);

   (b) was already in the Recipient's possession prior to disclosure by the Discloser;

   (c) is independently developed by the Recipient without reference to the Confidential Information;

   (d) is received by the Recipient from a third party who is not bound by obligations of confidentiality;

   (e) is required to be disclosed by law, regulation, or order of any court or regulatory authority.

3.2 The Recipient acknowledges and agrees that even if information becomes publicly available, the Recipient's obligation to treat such information as Confidential Information shall continue in full force and effect for the duration of this Agreement.

3.3 The Recipient shall not disclose Confidential Information pursuant to any legal or regulatory requirement without the prior written consent of the Discloser, regardless of any legal obligation or court order to do so. The Recipient shall resist any such requirement to the fullest extent permitted by law and shall bear all costs of doing so.

## 4. DURATION

4.1 **This Agreement shall continue in full force and effect in perpetuity.** The obligations of confidentiality shall not expire and shall continue to bind the Recipient and its successors, assigns, heirs, and personal representatives indefinitely, without limitation as to time.

4.2 There shall be no right for either Party to terminate this Agreement.

## 5. RETURN OF INFORMATION

5.1 Upon the written request of the Discloser at any time, the Recipient shall immediately:

   (a) return to the Discloser all documents and materials containing Confidential Information;

## 7. NON-COMPETE

7.1 **For a period of twenty-four (24) months from the date of this Agreement**, the Recipient shall not, directly or indirectly, whether alone or in conjunction with any other person:

   (a) carry on, be engaged in, be concerned with, or be interested in any business that competes with or is similar to any business carried on by the Discloser or any of its group companies;

   (b) solicit, canvass, approach, or deal with any person who is or has at any time during the twelve (12) months prior to the date of this Agreement been a client, customer, supplier, agent, or business contact of the Discloser or any of its group companies;

   (c) entice away, solicit, or endeavour to entice away from the Discloser any director, officer, employee, consultant, or agent of the Discloser or any of its group companies;

   (d) be employed by, provide services to, invest in, lend to, or otherwise assist any entity or individual that carries on a business which competes with any aspect of the Discloser's business.

7.2 The restrictions in clause 7.1 shall apply worldwide without geographic limitation.

7.3 Each sub-clause of clause 7.1 constitutes an entirely separate and independent restriction and the invalidity or unenforceability of any such restriction shall not affect the validity or enforceability of any of the remaining restrictions.
