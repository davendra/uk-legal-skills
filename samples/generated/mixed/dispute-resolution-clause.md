## 4. DISPUTE RESOLUTION — TIERED ADR PROCEDURE

### 4.1 Good Faith Negotiation

4.1.1 In the event of a Dispute, the Parties shall first attempt to resolve the Dispute by good faith negotiation.

4.1.2 Either Party may initiate the negotiation procedure by serving written notice on the other Party (a "Dispute Notice"), setting out in reasonable detail the nature of the Dispute and the relief sought.

4.1.3 Within 10 Business Days of receipt of a Dispute Notice, the senior project representatives of each Party (being the Client's Head of Asset Management and the Consultant's Managing Director) shall meet (in person or by video conference) to attempt to resolve the Dispute.

4.1.4 The Parties shall negotiate in good faith for a period of not less than 20 Business Days from the date of the Dispute Notice (the "Negotiation Period").

4.1.5 All communications made during the Negotiation Period shall be treated as without prejudice and shall not be admissible in any subsequent mediation, arbitration, or court proceedings.

### 4.2 Mediation

4.2.1 If the Dispute is not resolved by negotiation within the Negotiation Period, either Party may refer the Dispute to mediation.

4.2.2 Mediation shall be conducted under the **Centre for Effective Dispute Resolution (CEDR) Model Mediation Procedure** in force at the time of referral.

4.2.3 The mediator shall be appointed by agreement of the Parties within 10 Business Days of the referral to mediation. If the Parties are unable to agree on a mediator, either Party may apply to CEDR to appoint a mediator.

4.2.4 The mediation shall take place in London at a venue agreed by the Parties or, failing agreement, at CEDR's offices.

4.2.5 The Parties shall each bear their own costs of the mediation and shall share equally the mediator's fees and venue costs.

4.2.6 The mediation shall be completed within 42 days of referral. If the Dispute is not resolved within this period, either Party may proceed to arbitration under clause 4.3.

4.2.7 The mediation shall be confidential and without prejudice. Any settlement reached in mediation shall be recorded in a written settlement agreement signed by both Parties.

### 4.3 Arbitration

4.3.1 If the Dispute is not resolved by mediation (or if either Party fails to participate in mediation in good faith), either Party may refer the Dispute to binding arbitration.

4.3.2 The arbitration shall be administered by the **London Court of International Arbitration (LCIA)** under the **LCIA Arbitration Rules** in force at the date of commencement of the arbitration.

4.3.3 The tribunal shall consist of a sole arbitrator appointed by agreement of the Parties within 14 days of the commencement of the arbitration. If the Parties cannot agree, the arbitrator shall be appointed by the LCIA Court.

4.3.4 The seat of arbitration shall be London, England. The language of the arbitration shall be English.

4.3.5 The arbitrator shall have the power to:

(a) award damages (including interest);

(b) grant injunctive relief;

(c) make declarations;

(d) order specific performance;

(e) award costs.

4.3.6 The arbitrator's award shall be final and binding on the Parties and may be entered as a judgment in any court of competent jurisdiction.

4.3.7 The Parties agree that the arbitration proceedings and the award shall be confidential, save to the extent that disclosure is required by law or for the purpose of enforcement.

## 5. LIMITATION PERIOD

5.1 **Any claim arising out of or in connection with this Agreement, whether in contract, tort (including negligence), breach of statutory duty, or otherwise, must be commenced within 6 (six) months of the date on which the Party bringing the claim first became aware (or ought reasonably to have become aware) of the facts giving rise to the claim.**

5.2 For the avoidance of doubt, the limitation period in clause 5.1 applies to all claims under this Agreement, including claims for defective Services, professional negligence, breach of warranty, and claims for contribution or indemnity.

5.3 The limitation period in clause 5.1 shall be a contractual limitation period for the purposes of section 5 of the **Limitation Act 1980** and shall take precedence over any longer statutory limitation period that would otherwise apply.

5.4 Any claim not commenced within the limitation period specified in clause 5.1 shall be time-barred and the Party against whom such claim would have been made shall have an absolute defence.

## 7. EXPERT DETERMINATION

7.1 Any Dispute that is primarily of a technical or engineering nature (including disputes relating to the quality of the Services, the adequacy of technical reports, or the interpretation of engineering data) may, by agreement of both Parties, be referred to an independent expert for determination instead of arbitration.

7.2 The expert shall be a chartered engineer with not less than 20 years' experience in infrastructure engineering, appointed by agreement of the Parties or, failing agreement, by the President of the Institution of Civil Engineers (ICE).

7.3 The expert shall act as an expert and not as an arbitrator. The expert's determination shall be final and binding on the Parties, save in the case of manifest error or fraud.

7.4 The Parties shall share equally the costs of the expert determination, unless the expert determines otherwise.

8.1 **Costs:** Each Party shall bear its own legal and professional costs in connection with any Dispute, unless the arbitrator or court orders otherwise. 8.2 **Waiver:** No failure or delay by either Party in exercising any right or remedy under this Agreement shall operate as a waiver of that right or remedy.

8.3 **Severability:** If any provision of this Agreement is found to be invalid, illegal, or unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall continue in full force and effect.

8.4 **Entire Agreement:** This Agreement, together with its Schedules, constitutes the entire agreement between the Parties and supersedes all prior negotiations, representations, and agreements.

8.5 **Notices:** All notices under this Agreement shall be in writing and shall be delivered by hand, sent by first-class post, or sent by email to the addresses specified in Schedule 3.

8.6 **Third Party Rights:** No person who is not a party to this Agreement shall have any rights under the Contracts (Rights of Third Parties) Act 1999 to enforce any term of this Agreement.
