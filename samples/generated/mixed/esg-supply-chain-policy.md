## 1. Introduction and Purpose

1.1 Fortuna Consumer Goods plc ("Fortuna" or the "Company") is committed to responsible and sustainable supply chain management. This Supply Chain ESG Policy (the "Policy") sets out the Company's expectations for environmental, social, and governance ("ESG") standards across its supply chain.

1.2 Fortuna is a UK-listed public limited company (Company No. 04321098) with its registered office at Fortuna House, 88 Cheapside, London EC2V 6AX. The Company designs, manufactures, and distributes consumer goods (personal care, household cleaning, and food products) across the United Kingdom and 14 international markets.

1.3 This Policy applies to all suppliers, sub-contractors, agents, and business partners in the Company's supply chain (collectively, "Suppliers"), regardless of tier, geography, or commodity.

1.4 The Company's supply chain comprises approximately 2,400 direct (Tier 1) suppliers across 38 countries, with significant sourcing operations in the United Kingdom, Germany, Poland, Turkey, India, China, Indonesia, and Brazil.

---

## 2. Scope

2.1 This Policy covers the following ESG dimensions:

- **Environmental:** Climate change, carbon emissions, water stewardship, waste management, biodiversity, deforestation, chemical management, and circular economy
- **Social:** Human rights, labour standards, modern slavery, health and safety, diversity and inclusion, community impact, and living wages
- **Governance:** Anti-bribery and corruption, ethical business practices, data protection, tax transparency, and supply chain transparency

2.2 This Policy should be read in conjunction with the Company's:

- Modern Slavery Statement (published pursuant to section 54 of the Modern Slavery Act 2015)
- Anti-Bribery and Corruption Policy
- Environmental Policy
- Human Rights Policy
- Supplier Code of Conduct

## 3. Environmental Standards

### 3.1 Climate and Carbon

3.1.1 The Company has committed to achieving **net zero Scope 1 and 2 emissions by 2035** and **net zero across the full value chain (Scopes 1, 2, and 3) by 2050**, in line with the Science Based Targets initiative (SBTi).

3.1.2 Suppliers shall:

(a) measure and report their greenhouse gas emissions (Scopes 1 and 2 as a minimum) using the GHG Protocol Corporate Standard;

(b) set science-based emission reduction targets where feasible;

(c) participate in the Company's Scope 3 emissions data collection programme;

(d) prioritise energy efficiency measures and the use of renewable energy.

### 3.2 Water and Waste

3.2.1 Suppliers shall:

(a) implement water stewardship practices, including measurement of water consumption and wastewater management;

(b) minimise waste generation and maximise recycling and recovery rates;

(c) manage hazardous waste in compliance with all applicable regulations;

(d) eliminate the use of single-use plastics in packaging supplied to the Company by 2028.

### 3.3 Deforestation and Biodiversity

3.3.1 The Company is committed to **zero deforestation** across its supply chain by 2027.

3.3.2 Suppliers of palm oil, soy, cocoa, paper, and timber shall provide evidence of compliance with the Company's No Deforestation, No Peat, No Exploitation (NDPE) policy, including:

(a) certification under an approved scheme (RSPO, FSC, or equivalent);

(b) traceability to plantation level;

(c) compliance with the **Environment Act 2021** (Schedule 17 — forest risk commodities due diligence requirements).

## 4. Social Standards

### 4.1 Human Rights and Labour Standards

4.1.1 Suppliers shall respect and uphold internationally recognised human rights, including those set out in:

(a) the Universal Declaration of Human Rights;

(b) the International Labour Organisation (ILO) Declaration on Fundamental Principles and Rights at Work;

(c) the United Nations Guiding Principles on Business and Human Rights (UNGPs);

(d) the OECD Guidelines for Multinational Enterprises.

4.1.2 Suppliers shall not use forced, bonded, indentured, or involuntary labour, and shall not engage in human trafficking in any form.

4.1.3 Suppliers shall not use child labour. The minimum working age shall be the higher of 15 years, the local legal minimum age, or the age of completion of compulsory education.

### 4.2 Modern Slavery

4.2.1 The Company takes a zero-tolerance approach to modern slavery and expects all Suppliers to do the same.

4.2.2 Suppliers shall comply with the **Modern Slavery Act 2015** and any equivalent legislation in their jurisdiction.

4.2.3 Suppliers shall ensure that no recruitment fees or costs are charged to workers, in line with the Employer Pays Principle.

4.2.4 Suppliers shall have in place appropriate procedures for identifying, preventing, and mitigating modern slavery risks in their own operations and supply chains.

### 4.3 Health and Safety

4.3.1 Suppliers shall provide a safe and healthy working environment in compliance with all applicable laws and regulations, including the **Health and Safety at Work etc. Act 1974** (for UK-based suppliers).

4.3.2 Suppliers shall implement occupational health and safety management systems, conduct regular risk assessments, and provide appropriate training to all workers.

### 4.4 Living Wage

4.4.1 The Company is committed to ensuring living wages across its supply chain.

4.4.2 Suppliers should work towards paying their workers a living wage, benchmarked against recognised standards such as the Global Living Wage Coalition methodology or the Living Wage Foundation rates (for UK suppliers).

## 5. Governance Standards

### 5.1 Anti-Bribery and Corruption

5.1.1 Suppliers shall comply with the **Bribery Act 2010** and all applicable anti-bribery and corruption laws.

5.1.2 Suppliers shall not offer, promise, give, or accept any bribe, facilitation payment, or improper inducement, whether directly or indirectly.

5.1.3 Suppliers shall maintain adequate procedures to prevent bribery and corruption in their operations, in line with the Ministry of Justice Guidance on the Bribery Act 2010.

### 5.2 Data Protection

5.2.1 Suppliers shall comply with the **UK GDPR** and the **Data Protection Act 2018** (or equivalent data protection legislation in their jurisdiction) in respect of any personal data processed in connection with their relationship with the Company.

### 5.3 Tax Transparency

5.3.1 Suppliers shall comply with all applicable tax laws and shall not engage in aggressive tax avoidance schemes.

5.3.2 Suppliers shall co-operate with any requests from the Company for information regarding their tax practices, including country-by-country reporting where applicable.

---

## 6. Supplier Assessment and Engagement

### 6.1 Supplier Self-Assessment

6.1.1 All Tier 1 suppliers shall complete the Company's annual ESG Self-Assessment Questionnaire (SAQ), covering environmental, social, and governance practices.

6.1.2 The SAQ shall be used to assign each Supplier an ESG risk rating (Low, Medium, High, or Critical).

### 6.2 Due Diligence

6.2.1 The Company shall conduct enhanced ESG due diligence on suppliers rated as High or Critical risk.

6.2.2 Due diligence shall include a review of the Supplier's ESG policies, practices, and track record, and may include requests for documentation, certifications, and evidence of corrective actions.

### 6.3 Supplier Engagement

6.3.1 The Company is committed to a partnership approach and shall work collaboratively with Suppliers to build ESG capacity and drive continuous improvement.

6.3.2 The Company shall provide guidance, training, and support to help Suppliers meet the standards set out in this Policy.

---

## 7. Non-Compliance

7.1 The Company recognises that achieving compliance with this Policy is a journey and that some Suppliers may not meet all standards immediately.

7.2 Where non-compliance is identified, the Company shall work with the Supplier to agree a corrective action plan.

7.3 **The Company reserves the right to terminate its relationship with any Supplier that fails to comply with this Policy or to make reasonable progress towards compliance.** However, the Company acknowledges that termination of a supplier relationship should be a last resort and that engagement is generally preferred.

7.4 The Company shall not impose financial penalties on Suppliers for ESG non-compliance.

---

## 8. Reporting and Transparency

8.1 The Company shall report on supply chain ESG performance in its Annual Report and Accounts, in accordance with the requirements of:

(a) the **Companies Act 2006** (Strategic Report requirements, including section 414CB — climate-related financial disclosures);

(b) the **Task Force on Climate-related Financial Disclosures (TCFD)** recommendations;

(c) the **UK Modern Slavery Act 2015** (section 54);

(d) the **Environment Act 2021** (forest risk commodities);

(e) the **Corporate Sustainability Reporting Directive (CSRD)** (to the extent applicable).

8.2 The Company shall publish an annual ESG Supply Chain Report providing data on supplier assessments, audit findings, corrective actions, and progress against targets.

---

## 9. Governance and Oversight

9.1 The Board of Directors has ultimate responsibility for the Company's supply chain ESG performance.

9.2 The Chief Procurement Officer is responsible for the implementation and day-to-day management of this Policy.

9.3 The Sustainability Committee (a sub-committee of the Board) shall receive quarterly updates on supply chain ESG performance.

9.4 The Company's Internal Audit function shall include supply chain ESG in its annual audit plan.

---

## 10. Grievance and Whistleblowing

10.1 The Company maintains a confidential whistleblowing hotline (operated by Safecall Ltd) for the reporting of concerns relating to supply chain ESG issues, including human rights abuses, environmental damage, or corruption.

10.2 Reports may be made by any person, including Supplier employees, community members, and other stakeholders, without fear of retaliation.

10.3 The Company shall investigate all credible reports and take appropriate action.

---

## 11. Review

11.1 This Policy shall be reviewed annually by the Chief Procurement Officer and the Sustainability Committee, and approved by the Board of Directors.
