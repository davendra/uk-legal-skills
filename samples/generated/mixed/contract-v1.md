# IT SERVICES AGREEMENT

## Version 1.0

---

**DATE:** 1 September 2025

---

## PARTIES

1. **Brightwell Retail Group Ltd** (Company No. 08901234), whose registered office is at 40 Victoria Street, London SW1H 0EU (the "Client"); and

2. **Nextera Systems Ltd** (Company No. 10567890), whose registered office is at Nextera House, 5 Spinningfields, Manchester M3 3AP (the "Provider").

Together the "Parties" and each a "Party".

---

## BACKGROUND

(A) The Client is a multi-brand retail group operating 85 stores across England, Wales, and Scotland, with an e-commerce platform generating approximately GBP 120 million annual online revenue.

(B) The Provider is a specialist IT services company providing enterprise technology solutions, including e-commerce platform management, cloud infrastructure, and digital transformation services.

(C) The Client wishes to engage the Provider to provide IT services in connection with the Client's e-commerce platform and supporting technology infrastructure, and the Provider has agreed to provide such services on the terms set out in this Agreement.

---

## 1. DEFINITIONS

1.1 In this Agreement:

"Commencement Date" means 1 October 2025;

"Confidential Information" means all information of a confidential nature disclosed by one Party to the other in connection with this Agreement;

"Deliverables" means the outputs, reports, configurations, code, and other materials produced by the Provider in the course of providing the Services;

"Initial Term" means 36 months from the Commencement Date;

"Services" means the IT services described in Schedule 1;

"Service Levels" means the service level targets set out in Schedule 2;

"Working Day" means any day other than a Saturday, Sunday, or public holiday in England.

---

## 2. SERVICES

2.1 The Provider shall provide the Services to the Client during the Term with reasonable skill and care, in accordance with the Service Levels.

2.2 The Services shall include:

(a) management and maintenance of the Client's e-commerce platform (Magento/Adobe Commerce);

(b) cloud infrastructure management (AWS);

(c) application development and integration services;

(d) IT security monitoring and incident response;

(e) 24/7 helpdesk and technical support;

(f) data analytics and business intelligence support.

2.3 The Provider shall assign a dedicated team of no fewer than 8 full-time equivalent personnel to the Client's account.

---

## 3. FEES AND PAYMENT

3.1 The Client shall pay the Provider a monthly service fee of GBP 95,000 (ninety-five thousand pounds) exclusive of VAT for the Services described in Schedule 1.

3.2 Additional services outside the scope of Schedule 1 shall be charged at the following day rates:

| Role | Day Rate (excl. VAT) |
|------|---------------------|
| Technical Architect | GBP 1,400 |
| Senior Developer | GBP 1,100 |
| Developer | GBP 850 |
| Project Manager | GBP 950 |
| QA/Test Engineer | GBP 750 |

3.3 The Provider shall issue invoices monthly in advance for the service fee and monthly in arrears for any additional services.

3.4 **The Client shall pay each invoice within 90 (ninety) days of the date of the invoice.**

3.5 In the event of late payment, interest shall accrue at the rate of 8% per annum above the base rate of the Bank of England, calculated daily from the due date until the date of actual payment.

3.6 The Provider may not suspend the Services for non-payment unless the Client's account is more than 120 days overdue.

---

## 4. SERVICE LEVELS AND REMEDIES

4.1 The Provider shall deliver the Services in accordance with the Service Levels set out in Schedule 2.

4.2 In the event that the Provider fails to meet any Service Level in any calendar month, the Client shall be entitled to a service credit calculated as follows:

| Service Level Achievement | Service Credit |
|--------------------------|----------------|
| 95% - 99.9% | 5% of monthly fee |
| 90% - 94.9% | 10% of monthly fee |
| Below 90% | 15% of monthly fee |

4.3 Service credits shall be capped at 15% of the monthly service fee per calendar month.

4.4 Service credits shall be the Client's sole and exclusive remedy for failure to meet Service Levels.

---

## 5. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

5.1 All intellectual property rights in the Provider's pre-existing technology, tools, frameworks, and methodologies ("Provider IP") shall remain the property of the Provider.

5.2 **All intellectual property rights in any Deliverables created by the Provider in the course of providing the Services, including all bespoke code, configurations, integrations, designs, and documentation, shall vest in the Provider upon creation.** The Provider grants the Client a non-exclusive, non-transferable licence to use the Deliverables during the Term for the Client's internal business purposes only.

5.3 Upon termination or expiry of this Agreement, the licence granted under clause 5.2 shall continue for a transitional period of 6 months only, after which the Client shall cease all use of the Deliverables unless a separate licence is agreed.

5.4 The Client hereby assigns to the Provider all rights, title, and interest in any improvements, modifications, or derivative works created by the Provider that incorporate or are based upon the Provider's methodologies or frameworks, regardless of whether they were developed using the Client's specifications.

---

## 6. LIABILITY

6.1 Nothing in this Agreement excludes or limits either Party's liability for death or personal injury caused by negligence, fraud, or fraudulent misrepresentation.

6.2 **Subject to clause 6.1, the Provider's total aggregate liability under or in connection with this Agreement, whether in contract, tort (including negligence), breach of statutory duty, or otherwise, shall be unlimited.**

6.3 The Client acknowledges that the Provider's liability is not capped and that this reflects the critical nature of the Services to the Client's business operations.

6.4 Neither Party shall be liable for any indirect, consequential, or special loss, including loss of profit, loss of revenue, loss of data, loss of business opportunity, or loss of goodwill, except that this exclusion shall not apply to the Client's obligation to pay the fees due under this Agreement.

---

## 7. CONFIDENTIALITY

7.1 Each Party shall keep confidential all Confidential Information of the other Party and shall not disclose it to any third party without the prior written consent of the disclosing Party.

7.2 The obligations of confidentiality shall not apply to information that:

(a) is or becomes publicly available through no fault of the receiving Party;

(b) was already known to the receiving Party prior to disclosure;

(c) is required to be disclosed by law or regulation.

7.3 The obligations of confidentiality shall survive the termination of this Agreement for a period of 3 years.

---

## 8. DATA PROTECTION

8.1 The Provider shall comply with all applicable data protection legislation, including the UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018, in the performance of the Services.

8.2 To the extent that the Provider processes personal data on behalf of the Client, a separate Data Processing Agreement shall be entered into by the Parties.

---

## 9. TERMINATION

9.1 Either Party may terminate this Agreement immediately by written notice if the other Party:

(a) commits a material breach which is not remedied within 30 days of written notice specifying the breach;

(b) becomes insolvent, enters administration, has a receiver appointed, or passes a resolution for winding up.

9.2 **This Agreement does not include a right of termination for convenience. Neither Party may terminate this Agreement during the Initial Term except in accordance with clause 9.1.**

9.3 Following the Initial Term, this Agreement shall continue on a rolling 12-month basis and may only be terminated by either Party giving 12 months' written notice, such notice not to expire before the end of the then-current 12-month period.

9.4 Upon termination for any reason:

(a) the Provider shall provide transition assistance for a period of up to 6 months, charged at the Provider's standard day rates;

(b) the Client shall pay all outstanding fees, including fees for the remainder of the notice period;

(c) the Provider shall return or destroy all Client data within 60 days of the effective date of termination.

---

## 10. FORCE MAJEURE

10.1 Neither Party shall be liable for any failure or delay in performing its obligations under this Agreement to the extent that such failure or delay is caused by a Force Majeure Event.

10.2 "Force Majeure Event" means any event beyond a Party's reasonable control, including acts of God, fire, flood, earthquake, epidemic, pandemic, war, terrorism, industrial action, or failure of third-party telecommunications or power supply.

---

## 11. DISPUTE RESOLUTION

11.1 Any dispute arising under this Agreement shall first be referred to the Parties' respective account managers for resolution.

11.2 If not resolved within 20 Working Days, the dispute shall be escalated to a director of each Party.

11.3 If not resolved within a further 20 Working Days, either Party may commence proceedings in the courts of England and Wales.

---

## 12. GENERAL

12.1 This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the Parties.

12.2 No amendment shall be effective unless in writing and signed by both Parties.

12.3 The Provider may sub-contract any of its obligations under this Agreement without the Client's prior consent, provided that the Provider shall remain liable for the acts and omissions of its sub-contractors.

12.4 This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of England and Wales.

12.5 The Parties submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of England and Wales.
