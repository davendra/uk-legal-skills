## RECITALS

(A) The Partners wish to carry on business together in partnership under the name "Apex Advisory Partners" (the "Firm") providing management consulting, strategy advisory, and digital transformation services.

(B) The Partners wish to record the terms upon which the partnership is to be conducted.

(C) This Agreement is intended to govern the rights and obligations of the Partners inter se.

---

## 1. NAME AND BUSINESS

1.1 The Partners shall carry on business in partnership under the name "Apex Advisory Partners" or such other name as may be agreed by the Partners from time to time.

1.2 The principal business of the Firm shall be the provision of management consulting, strategy advisory, digital transformation, and related professional services.

1.3 The Firm shall not engage in any business outside the scope described in clause 1.2 without the agreement of the Partners.

## 2. COMMENCEMENT AND DURATION

2.1 The Partnership commenced on 1 April 2026 and shall continue until dissolved in accordance with this Agreement or by operation of law.

2.2 The Partnership Act 1890 shall apply to this Partnership except to the extent that its provisions are expressly modified or excluded by this Agreement.

## 3. CAPITAL CONTRIBUTIONS

3.1 The Partners shall contribute the following amounts as initial capital:

| Partner | Capital Contribution |
|---------|---------------------|
| Partner A (Sarah Mitchell) | GBP 100,000 |
| Partner B (James Okonkwo) | GBP 50,000 |
| Partner C (Dr. Priya Sharma) | GBP 25,000 |

3.2 Capital contributions shall be paid into the Firm's designated bank account within 14 days of the commencement date.

3.3 No interest shall be payable on capital contributions unless otherwise agreed by the Partners.

3.4 No Partner shall be required to contribute further capital without the agreement of all Partners.

## 4. PROFIT AND LOSS SHARING

4.1 The net profits and losses of the Firm shall be divided equally among the Partners in the following proportions:

| Partner | Share |
|---------|-------|
| Partner A (Sarah Mitchell) | One-third (33.33%) |
| Partner B (James Okonkwo) | One-third (33.33%) |
| Partner C (Dr. Priya Sharma) | One-third (33.33%) |

4.2 Profits shall be calculated after deduction of all proper expenses of the Firm and provision for taxation.

4.3 Partners may draw against their anticipated share of profits on a monthly basis, subject to a maximum drawing of GBP 8,000 per month, or such other amount as may be agreed.

4.4 Annual profit distributions shall be made within 90 days of the end of each accounting year, following completion and approval of the annual accounts.

## 5. MANAGEMENT AND DECISION-MAKING

5.1 The Partners shall participate in the management of the Firm and each Partner shall devote substantially the whole of their working time and attention to the business of the Firm.

5.2 Decisions relating to the ordinary course of business may be made by any Partner acting alone.

5.3 The following matters shall require the agreement of the Partners:

(a) admission of a new Partner;

(b) expulsion of a Partner;

(c) borrowing in excess of GBP 25,000;

(d) entering into any contract with a value exceeding GBP 50,000;

(e) acquisition or disposal of any capital asset exceeding GBP 10,000;

(f) any change to the nature of the Firm's business;

(g) any litigation brought in the name of the Firm.

## 6. BANKING AND ACCOUNTS

6.1 The Firm shall maintain a bank account in the name of the Firm with a UK clearing bank.

6.2 Cheques and electronic payments exceeding GBP 10,000 shall require the signature or authorisation of at least two Partners.

6.3 Proper books of account shall be kept at the Firm's principal place of business and shall be open to inspection by any Partner at all reasonable times.

6.4 The Firm's accounts shall be prepared annually to 5 April by a qualified accountant appointed by the Partners.

6.5 Each Partner shall be entitled to a copy of the annual accounts within 3 months of the end of the accounting year.

## 7. PROPERTY

7.1 All property and assets acquired by the Firm shall be partnership property and shall be held and applied exclusively for the purposes of the partnership.

7.2 Any intellectual property created by a Partner in the course of the Firm's business shall be partnership property.

## 8. DUTIES OF PARTNERS

8.1 Each Partner shall:

(a) act in good faith and in the best interests of the Firm;

(b) give a true account of all dealings and transactions relating to the Firm;

(c) not engage in any business that competes with the Firm without the written consent of the other Partners;

(d) not assign, charge, or otherwise deal with their share in the Firm without the written consent of the other Partners;

(e) comply with all applicable laws and professional standards.

## 9. RETIREMENT AND EXPULSION

9.1 A Partner may retire from the Firm by giving not less than 6 months' written notice to the other Partners.

9.2 A Partner may be expelled from the Firm by the unanimous decision of the other Partners on the grounds of:

(a) material breach of this Agreement;

(b) conduct prejudicial to the business of the Firm;

(c) bankruptcy or entry into an individual voluntary arrangement;

(d) permanent incapacity preventing the Partner from performing their duties for a continuous period of 6 months.

## 10. DISSOLUTION

10.1 The Partnership shall be dissolved:

(a) by the agreement of all Partners;

(b) upon the death or bankruptcy of any Partner, unless the remaining Partners elect to continue within 30 days;

(c) by order of the court under section 35 of the Partnership Act 1890.

10.2 Upon dissolution, the assets of the Firm shall be applied in the following order:

(a) payment of the debts and liabilities of the Firm to third parties;

(b) repayment of any loans made by Partners to the Firm;

(c) repayment of Partners' capital contributions;

(d) distribution of the surplus (if any) among the Partners in accordance with their profit-sharing ratios.

## 11. RESTRICTIVE COVENANTS

11.1 For a period of 12 months following retirement or expulsion, a Partner shall not:

(a) solicit or deal with any client of the Firm with whom the Partner had material contact during the 12 months preceding departure;

(b) solicit or entice away any employee or Partner of the Firm.

11.2 The restrictions in clause 11.1 shall apply within the United Kingdom only.

## 12. CONFIDENTIALITY

12.1 Each Partner shall keep confidential all information relating to the business, clients, and affairs of the Firm, both during the partnership and after leaving.

## 13. DISPUTE RESOLUTION

13.1 Any dispute between the Partners shall first be referred to mediation under the CEDR Model Mediation Procedure.

13.2 If the dispute is not resolved within 30 days of referral to mediation, either Party may refer the dispute to arbitration under the rules of the London Court of International Arbitration.

## 14. NOTICES

14.1 Any notice under this Agreement shall be in writing and delivered by hand, first-class post, or email to the Partner's address set out above (or such other address as may be notified in writing).

## 15. GOVERNING LAW

15.1 This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of England and Wales.

15.2 The Partners submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of England and Wales.
