## 14. Key Risks and Recommendations

### 14.1 Critical Issues

1. **IP Register (IP-001):** No IP register has been provided despite being requested over 3 weeks ago. IP valuation is a key component of the transaction price. **Recommend: completion condition.**

2. **Litigation Summary (LIT-001):** No litigation summary provided. One ET claim known but no details. Product liability risk is elevated in the aerospace sector. **Recommend: enhanced seller warranties and specific indemnity for undisclosed claims.**

3. **Tax Computations (TAX-002):** Cannot complete tax due diligence without computations. R&D tax relief claims cannot be verified. **Recommend: tax indemnity with low de minimis threshold.**

### 14.2 Other Issues

4. **Change of Control Clauses:** The Rolls-Royce and BAE Systems contracts contain change of control provisions. Consent from both customers will be required prior to or promptly following completion.

5. **Lease Rent Review (PROP-001):** The October 2025 rent review is overdue. The outcome could affect occupancy costs.

6. **Employment Tribunal Claim (EMP-004):** Details required to assess liability and any impact on employee relations.
