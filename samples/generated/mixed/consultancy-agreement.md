# CONSULTANCY AGREEMENT

---

**DATE:** 3 March 2026

---

## PARTIES

1. **FinanceFirst Bank plc** (Company No. 01234567), whose registered office is at 1 Threadneedle Court, London, EC2R 8AH (the "Client"); and

2. **InfoSec Consulting Ltd** (Company No. 12345678), whose registered office is at 15 Cathedral Close, Guildford, GU1 3EQ (the "Consultant").

---

## BACKGROUND

(A) The Client is a UK-authorised bank regulated by the Financial Conduct Authority (FCA) and the Prudential Regulation Authority (PRA).

(B) The Consultant is a specialist information security consultancy providing cyber security, penetration testing, and security architecture services.

(C) The Client wishes to engage the Consultant to provide information security advisory and implementation services, and the Consultant has agreed to provide such services on the terms set out in this Agreement.

---

## 1. DEFINITIONS

1.1 In this Agreement:

"Business Day" means any day other than a Saturday, Sunday, or public holiday in England;

"Commencement Date" means 1 April 2026;

"Confidential Information" means all information (whether written, oral, or in electronic form) relating to the business, affairs, operations, customers, processes, budgets, plans, or technology of either Party that is not in the public domain;

"Deliverables" means the reports, assessments, documentation, configurations, and other outputs produced by the Consultant in the course of providing the Services;

"Engagement Period" means the period from the Commencement Date to 30 September 2027, unless terminated earlier in accordance with clause 10;

"Key Personnel" means Marcus Webb (Lead Security Architect) and any replacement approved under clause 3.5;

"Services" means the information security services described in Schedule 1.

## 2. ENGAGEMENT

2.1 The Client engages the Consultant, and the Consultant agrees, to provide the Services during the Engagement Period on the terms set out in this Agreement.

2.2 This Agreement does not create a relationship of employment, agency, or partnership between the Parties. The Consultant is an independent contractor.

2.3 Nothing in this Agreement shall render the Consultant or any of its personnel an employee, worker, agent, or partner of the Client, and the Consultant shall not hold itself out as such.

## 3. PROVISION OF SERVICES

3.1 The Consultant shall provide the Services with reasonable skill, care, and diligence, consistent with the standards expected of a competent information security professional.

3.2 The Services shall be provided primarily through Marcus Webb (Lead Security Architect), who shall be the Key Personnel for this engagement.

3.3 **The Consultant shall provide the Services at the Client's premises at 1 Threadneedle Court, London, EC2R 8AH for a minimum of 3 (three) days per week (typically Tuesday to Thursday).** The remaining days may be worked remotely at the Consultant's discretion.

3.4 The Consultant shall comply with all reasonable security policies, access procedures, and site rules of the Client when working at the Client's premises.

3.5 **The Consultant may substitute Marcus Webb with an alternative consultant of equivalent qualifications and experience, provided that the Client has given its prior written approval of such substitution (such approval not to be unreasonably withheld or delayed).** The substitute must hold current SC security clearance.

3.6 The Consultant shall maintain, at its own expense:

(a) professional indemnity insurance with a minimum cover of GBP 2,000,000 per claim;

(b) public liability insurance with a minimum cover of GBP 1,000,000 per claim;

(c) cyber liability insurance with a minimum cover of GBP 1,000,000 per claim.

3.7 The Consultant shall provide copies of insurance certificates to the Client upon request.

## 4. CLIENT OBLIGATIONS

4.1 The Client shall:

(a) provide the Consultant with reasonable access to its premises, systems, personnel, and information as required for the provision of the Services;

(b) designate a Client contact person to act as the primary liaison with the Consultant;

(c) provide timely feedback and approvals as reasonably required;

(d) ensure that the Consultant has appropriate security clearances and access credentials.

4.2 The Client shall provide the Consultant with a dedicated desk, network access, and building access pass when working on the Client's premises.

## 5. FEES AND PAYMENT

5.1 The Client shall pay the Consultant a day rate of GBP 1,200 (one thousand two hundred pounds) exclusive of VAT for each day on which the Services are provided.

5.2 A day shall be defined as 8 hours of work. Hours worked beyond 8 hours in a single day shall be charged at a pro-rata rate.

5.3 The Consultant shall submit monthly invoices in arrears, itemising the days worked and a summary of Services provided.

5.4 The Client shall pay each invoice within 30 days of receipt.

5.5 The Consultant shall be responsible for its own travel and subsistence costs, except where travel is required to a location other than the Client's premises at 1 Threadneedle Court.

5.6 The Consultant shall be responsible for accounting for and paying all tax, National Insurance, and VAT due in respect of fees received under this Agreement.

## 6. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

6.1 All Deliverables produced specifically for the Client in the course of providing the Services shall be the property of the Client upon payment in full.

6.2 The Consultant retains ownership of all pre-existing intellectual property, tools, methodologies, frameworks, and know-how ("Consultant IP"). Where Consultant IP is incorporated into any Deliverable, the Consultant grants the Client a non-exclusive, perpetual, royalty-free licence to use such Consultant IP solely in connection with the Deliverables.

6.3 Nothing in this Agreement shall transfer ownership of Consultant IP to the Client.

## 7. CONFIDENTIALITY

7.1 Each Party shall keep confidential all Confidential Information of the other Party and shall not disclose it to any third party without the prior written consent of the disclosing Party.

7.2 The obligations of confidentiality shall not apply to information that:

(a) is or becomes publicly available through no fault of the receiving Party;

(b) was already known to the receiving Party prior to disclosure;

(c) is required to be disclosed by law, regulation, or order of a court or regulatory authority (including the FCA or PRA).

7.3 The Consultant acknowledges that, given the Client's regulated status, the Consultant may have access to information that is subject to regulatory obligations, including market-sensitive information, and the Consultant shall handle such information with the highest degree of care.

7.4 The obligations of confidentiality shall survive the termination or expiry of this Agreement for a period of 5 years.

## 8. DATA PROTECTION

8.1 To the extent that the Consultant processes personal data on behalf of the Client, the Parties shall comply with a separate Data Processing Agreement.

8.2 The Consultant shall comply with all applicable data protection legislation, including the UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018.

## 9. LIABILITY

9.1 Nothing in this Agreement excludes or limits either Party's liability for death or personal injury caused by negligence, fraud or fraudulent misrepresentation, or any other liability that cannot be excluded by law.

9.2 Subject to clause 9.1, the Consultant's total aggregate liability under or in connection with this Agreement shall not exceed the greater of (a) GBP 500,000 and (b) the total fees paid under this Agreement in the 12-month period preceding the event giving rise to the claim.

9.3 Neither Party shall be liable for any indirect, consequential, or special damages, including loss of profits, revenue, data, or business opportunity.

## 10. TERMINATION

10.1 Either Party may terminate this Agreement:

(a) by giving 30 days' written notice to the other Party;

(b) immediately by written notice if the other Party commits a material breach which is not remedied within 14 days of written notice specifying the breach;

(c) immediately by written notice if the other Party becomes insolvent, enters administration, or has a receiver appointed.

10.2 Upon termination, the Consultant shall:

(a) deliver to the Client all completed and partially completed Deliverables;

(b) return or securely destroy all Confidential Information of the Client;

(c) provide reasonable assistance for a handover period of up to 10 Business Days, to be charged at the day rate specified in clause 5.1.

10.3 Termination shall not affect any rights or obligations which have accrued prior to termination.

## 11. REGULATORY COMPLIANCE

11.1 The Consultant shall comply with all applicable FCA and PRA rules to the extent they apply to outsourced or third-party arrangements with regulated firms.

11.2 The Consultant acknowledges that the FCA and PRA may require access to the Client's records, including records held by or relating to the Consultant, and the Consultant shall co-operate with any such request.

## 12. DISPUTE RESOLUTION

12.1 Any dispute arising out of or in connection with this Agreement shall first be referred to the senior management of each Party for resolution.

12.2 If the dispute is not resolved within 30 days, either Party may refer the matter to mediation under the CEDR Model Mediation Procedure.

12.3 Nothing in this clause shall prevent either Party from seeking urgent injunctive or equitable relief.

## 13. GENERAL

13.1 This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the Parties and supersedes all prior discussions, negotiations, and agreements.

13.2 No amendment to this Agreement shall be effective unless made in writing and signed by both Parties.

13.3 The Consultant shall not assign or sub-contract any of its obligations under this Agreement without the prior written consent of the Client.

13.4 If any provision of this Agreement is found to be invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall continue in full force and effect.

## 14. GOVERNING LAW AND JURISDICTION

14.1 This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of England and Wales.

14.2 The Parties submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of England and Wales.
