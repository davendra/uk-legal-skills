## 1. COMMENCEMENT AND DURATION

1.1 Your employment with the Employer commenced on [start date] and shall continue until terminated by either party in accordance with clause 10 of this Agreement.

1.2 No period of previous employment counts towards your period of continuous employment with the Employer.

## 2. NATURE OF ENGAGEMENT

2.1 You are employed on a zero-hours basis. **The Employer is under no obligation to offer you any minimum number of hours of work, and you are under no obligation to accept any hours offered.**

2.2 When work is available, the Employer will contact you to offer shifts. You may accept or decline any shift offered without adverse consequence.

2.3 Your status under this agreement is that of a "worker" within the meaning of section 230(3) of the Employment Rights Act 1996.

## 3. PLACE OF WORK

3.1 Your normal place of work will be at any of the Employer's hospitality venues across the West Midlands region, as notified to you from time to time.

3.2 You may be required to work at different locations depending on operational requirements.

## 4. REMUNERATION

4.1 You will be paid the National Living Wage, currently GBP 12.21 per hour (as at April 2025), or such higher rate as may be agreed.

4.2 Payment will be made monthly in arrears by BACS transfer on or around the 28th of each calendar month.

4.3 You will receive an itemised payslip with each payment.

## 5. HOURS OF WORK

5.1 Your hours of work will vary depending on the shifts you accept.

5.2 The Employer will endeavour to provide you with reasonable notice of available shifts, normally not less than 48 hours in advance.

5.3 You are entitled to refuse any shift without giving a reason and without detriment.

## 6. HOLIDAY ENTITLEMENT

6.1 You are entitled to paid annual leave calculated on a pro-rata basis in accordance with the Working Time Regulations 1998.

6.2 Your holiday entitlement is 5.6 weeks per leave year, accrued on the basis of 12.07% of hours worked.

6.3 The holiday year runs from 1 January to 31 December.

6.4 Holiday pay will be calculated based on the average pay received over the previous 52 weeks in which you worked (excluding weeks in which no pay was received), in accordance with the Employment Rights Act 1996.

## 7. SICKNESS AND ABSENCE

7.1 If you are unable to attend a shift you have accepted, you must notify the Employer as soon as reasonably practicable and in any event before the shift is due to commence.

7.2 **Statutory Sick Pay (SSP) will be payable from the fourth qualifying day of any period of incapacity for work**, subject to you meeting the qualifying conditions.

7.3 You must provide a self-certification form for absences of up to 7 calendar days and a fit note from your GP for absences exceeding 7 calendar days.

7.4 The Employer does not operate a contractual sick pay scheme.

## 8. PENSION

8.1 You will be automatically enrolled in the Employer's workplace pension scheme in accordance with the Pensions Act 2008, provided you meet the qualifying criteria.

8.2 The Employer will contribute the minimum required percentage of qualifying earnings.

## 9. CONDUCT AND PERFORMANCE

9.1 You are expected to maintain satisfactory standards of conduct and performance at all times whilst working for the Employer.

9.2 The Employer's disciplinary and grievance procedures are available on request.

## 10. TERMINATION

10.1 During the first month of engagement, either party may terminate this agreement by giving one week's written notice.

10.2 After the first month, the Employer shall give:
- One week's notice for each complete year of continuous service, up to a maximum of 12 weeks.

10.3 After the first month, the Worker shall give one week's written notice.

10.4 The Employer reserves the right to terminate without notice in cases of gross misconduct.

## 11. CONFIDENTIALITY

11.1 You shall not, either during your engagement or after its termination, disclose to any person any confidential information relating to the business, affairs, customers, or suppliers of the Employer.

## 12. DATA PROTECTION

12.1 The Employer will process your personal data in accordance with the UK General Data Protection Regulation and the Data Protection Act 2018. A copy of the Employer's privacy notice for employees is available upon request.

## 13. EXCLUSIVITY

13.1 The Employer acknowledges that, given the nature of this zero-hours arrangement, you are free to work for other employers, and nothing in this Agreement shall restrict your ability to do so.

## 14. GOVERNING LAW

14.1 This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of England and Wales.

14.2 Any disputes arising under this Agreement shall be subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of the Employment Tribunal and the courts of England and Wales.
