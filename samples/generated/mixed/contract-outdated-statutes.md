## BACKGROUND

(A) The Client operates a group of private healthcare clinics across England and Wales, providing specialist medical, dental, and mental health services.

(B) The Provider is a managed IT services company specialising in healthcare technology infrastructure, cloud services, and data management.

(C) The Client wishes to engage the Provider to deliver managed IT services, and the Provider has agreed to provide such services on the terms set out in this Agreement.

---

## 1. DEFINITIONS

1.1 In this Agreement:

"Commencement Date" means 1 February 2026;

"Confidential Information" means all information of a confidential nature disclosed by one Party to the other, whether orally, in writing, or in electronic form;

"Data Protection Legislation" means the **Data Protection Act 1998** and any regulations made thereunder, and any applicable guidance issued by the Information Commissioner's Office;

"Equipment" means any hardware, software, or network infrastructure provided by the Provider;

"Initial Term" means 36 months from the Commencement Date;

"Services" means the managed IT services described in Schedule 1;

"Service Levels" means the service level commitments set out in Schedule 2;

"Working Day" means any day other than a Saturday, Sunday, or public holiday in England.

---

## 2. SERVICES

2.1 The Provider shall provide the Services to the Client during the Term with reasonable skill, care, and diligence, in accordance with the Service Levels.

2.2 The Services shall include:

(a) management and maintenance of the Client's IT infrastructure, including servers, network equipment, and end-user devices;

(b) cloud hosting and management of the Client's electronic patient record (EPR) system;

(c) IT helpdesk support (8am to 8pm, Monday to Saturday);

(d) cybersecurity monitoring, patch management, and incident response;

(e) data backup, disaster recovery, and business continuity services;

(f) strategic IT advisory services (quarterly technology review meetings).

2.3 The Provider shall allocate a dedicated account manager and a named service delivery manager to the Client's account.

---

## 3. DATA PROTECTION

3.1 The Parties acknowledge that in the course of providing the Services, the Provider will process personal data (including sensitive personal data as defined in the **Data Protection Act 1998**) on behalf of the Client.

3.2 The Provider shall process personal data only in accordance with the Client's instructions and shall comply with the **Data Protection Act 1998** and the **seventh data protection principle** (appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect against unauthorised or unlawful processing and against accidental loss, destruction, or damage).

3.3 The Provider shall ensure that its employees who have access to personal data are subject to appropriate obligations of confidentiality.

3.4 The Provider agrees to comply with the provisions of the **EU General Data Protection Regulation (Regulation (EU) 2016/679)** ("GDPR") to the extent applicable to the processing of personal data under this Agreement.

3.5 The Provider shall notify the Client promptly of any personal data breach and shall cooperate with the Client in respect of any notification obligations under the Data Protection Legislation.

3.6 Where personal data is transferred outside the European Economic Area, the Provider shall ensure that appropriate safeguards are in place in accordance with the **GDPR**, including the use of **EU Standard Contractual Clauses** approved by the European Commission.

---

## 4. REGULATORY COMPLIANCE

4.1 The Provider acknowledges that the Client is subject to regulation by the **Care Quality Commission (CQC)** and shall cooperate with any CQC inspection or information request that relates to the Services.

4.2 The Provider shall comply with all applicable laws and regulations in the performance of the Services, including:

(a) the **Data Protection Act 1998**;

(b) the **Computer Misuse Act 1990**;

(c) the **Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations 2003** (as amended);

(d) the **Health and Social Care Act 2012**;

(e) the NHS Data Security and Protection Toolkit requirements (to the extent applicable to the Client's operations).

4.3 The Provider shall ensure that all staff who access patient data have completed Information Governance training in accordance with the requirements of the **Caldicott Principles** and the **Common Law Duty of Confidentiality**.

## 7. FEES AND PAYMENT

7.1 The Client shall pay the Provider a monthly service fee of GBP 18,500 (eighteen thousand five hundred pounds) exclusive of VAT.

## 9. LIABILITY

9.1 Nothing in this Agreement excludes or limits either Party's liability for death or personal injury caused by negligence, fraud, or any liability which cannot be excluded by law.

9.2 Subject to clause 9.1, the Provider's total aggregate liability under this Agreement shall not exceed an amount equal to 100% of the total fees paid by the Client in the 12-month period preceding the event giving rise to the claim.

9.3 Neither Party shall be liable for any indirect, consequential, or special loss, including loss of profit, loss of data, loss of business, or loss of reputation.

---

## 10. TERM AND TERMINATION

10.1 This Agreement shall commence on the Commencement Date and shall continue for the Initial Term, unless terminated earlier in accordance with this clause.

10.2 Following the Initial Term, this Agreement shall continue on a rolling 12-month basis unless either Party gives at least 6 months' written notice of termination.

10.3 Either Party may terminate this Agreement immediately by written notice if the other Party:

(a) commits a material breach which is not remedied within 30 days of written notice;

(b) becomes insolvent, enters administration, or has a receiver or liquidator appointed.

10.4 Upon termination, the Provider shall:

(a) provide reasonable transition assistance for a period of up to 90 days;

(b) return or securely destroy all Client data and Confidential Information;

(c) provide the Client with a complete copy of all Client data in a standard, machine-readable format.

---

## 11. DISPUTE RESOLUTION

11.1 Any dispute arising out of or in connection with this Agreement shall be resolved in accordance with the following procedure:

(a) the dispute shall first be referred to the account manager and the Client's IT Director for resolution within 14 Working Days;

(b) if not resolved, the dispute shall be escalated to a senior executive of each Party for resolution within a further 14 Working Days;

(c) if not resolved, either Party may refer the dispute to mediation under the CEDR Model Mediation Procedure;

(d) nothing in this clause shall prevent either Party from seeking urgent relief from the courts.

---
