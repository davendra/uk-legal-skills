## 1. DEFINITIONS

In this Agreement:

"the Act" means the Housing Act 1988 (as amended by the Housing Act 1996 and the Renters' Rights Act 2025);

"the Deposit" means the sum specified in clause 4;

"the Property" means the dwelling described above;

"the Rent" means the sum specified in clause 3;

"the Term" means the period specified in clause 2.

## 2. TERM

2.1 This Agreement creates an assured shorthold tenancy for an initial fixed term of 12 months commencing on 1 February 2026 and ending on 31 January 2027.

2.2 At the end of the fixed term, this tenancy will continue as a periodic tenancy on a monthly basis, subject to the same terms and conditions, until terminated in accordance with this Agreement or by operation of law.

## 3. RENT

3.1 The Rent is GBP 1,150 (one thousand one hundred and fifty pounds) per calendar month, payable in advance on the 1st day of each month.

3.2 The first payment of Rent is due on or before the commencement of the Term.

3.3 Rent shall be paid by standing order to the Landlord's designated bank account, details of which will be provided separately.

3.4 The Landlord may increase the Rent during the periodic tenancy by giving not less than 2 months' notice in writing, using the procedure under section 13 of the Act.

## 4. DEPOSIT

4.1 The Tenant shall pay a deposit of GBP 1,150 (one thousand one hundred and fifty pounds), equivalent to five weeks' Rent, upon signing this Agreement.

4.2 **The Deposit will be protected in a government-authorised tenancy deposit scheme, namely the Deposit Protection Service (DPS)**, within 30 days of receipt.

4.3 The Landlord shall provide the Tenant with the prescribed information about the deposit protection within 30 days of receipt of the Deposit, as required by the Housing Act 2004.

4.4 The Deposit shall be returned to the Tenant at the end of the tenancy, subject to any lawful deductions for:

(a) unpaid rent;

(b) damage to the Property beyond fair wear and tear;

(c) missing items from the Inventory;

(d) cleaning costs where the Property is not returned in a reasonably clean condition.

## 5. LANDLORD'S OBLIGATIONS

5.1 The Landlord shall:

(a) keep in repair the structure and exterior of the Property, including drains, gutters, and external pipes (section 11, Landlord and Tenant Act 1985);

(b) keep in repair and proper working order the installations in the Property for the supply of water, gas, electricity, sanitation, and heating;

(c) ensure the Property is fit for human habitation throughout the tenancy (Homes (Fitness for Human Habitation) Act 2018);

(d) provide a valid **Energy Performance Certificate (EPC) with a rating of C or above** in compliance with the Minimum Energy Efficiency Standards;

(e) provide a current **Gas Safety Certificate** (Gas Safety (Installation and Use) Regulations 1998) prior to the commencement of the tenancy and annually thereafter;

(f) ensure that smoke alarms and carbon monoxide alarms are installed and in working order at the commencement of the tenancy (Smoke and Carbon Monoxide Alarm (Amendment) Regulations 2022);

(g) carry out electrical safety inspections at intervals of no more than 5 years and provide a copy of the Electrical Installation Condition Report (EICR) to the Tenant.

## 6. TENANT'S OBLIGATIONS

6.1 The Tenant shall:

(a) pay the Rent on time and in the manner specified;

(b) keep the Property in a clean and tidy condition;

(c) not cause or permit any damage to the Property beyond fair wear and tear;

(d) not make any structural alterations or additions to the Property without the Landlord's prior written consent;

(e) not use the Property for any illegal or immoral purpose;

(f) not keep any pets at the Property without the Landlord's prior written consent (such consent not to be unreasonably withheld);

(g) allow the Landlord access to the Property to inspect its condition or carry out repairs, subject to at least 24 hours' written notice (except in an emergency);

(h) not sublet or part with possession of the Property or any part of it without the Landlord's prior written consent;

(i) notify the Landlord promptly of any disrepair or defect in the Property.

## 7. TERMINATION

7.1 **During the fixed term**, neither party may terminate this Agreement except:

(a) by mutual written agreement; or

(b) by the Landlord exercising a break clause with 2 months' notice, for grounds specified under Schedule 2 of the Housing Act 1988.

7.2 **After the fixed term**, the Tenant may terminate the periodic tenancy by giving not less than one month's written notice, expiring at the end of a rental period.

7.3 **The Landlord may seek possession of the Property by serving a Section 21 notice under the Housing Act 1988, giving not less than 2 months' notice.** The Landlord must comply with all prescribed requirements before serving a Section 21 notice, including protecting the Deposit and providing the required certificates.

7.4 The Landlord may also seek possession on one or more of the grounds set out in Schedule 2 of the Housing Act 1988, by serving a Section 8 notice.

## 8. INSURANCE

8.1 The Landlord shall insure the Property and the building of which it forms part against fire and other standard perils.

8.2 The Tenant shall be responsible for insuring their own contents and personal belongings.

## 9. COUNCIL TAX AND UTILITIES

9.1 The Tenant shall be responsible for paying council tax and all charges for gas, electricity, water, telephone, broadband, and television licence attributable to the Property during the tenancy.

## 10. DATA PROTECTION

10.1 The Landlord will process the Tenant's personal data in accordance with the UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018. The Landlord's privacy notice is available at martinproperties.co.uk/privacy.

## 11. GOVERNING LAW

11.1 This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of England and Wales.

11.2 The parties submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of England and Wales.
