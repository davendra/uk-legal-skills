## BACKGROUND

(A) The Parties entered into an IT Services Agreement dated 1 September 2025 (the "Original Agreement").

(B) The Parties wish to amend and restate the Original Agreement on the terms set out in this Agreement, with effect from the Effective Date.

(C) This Agreement supersedes and replaces the Original Agreement in its entirety with effect from the Effective Date.

---

## 1. DEFINITIONS

1.1 In this Agreement:

"Commencement Date" means 1 October 2025 (being the commencement date under the Original Agreement);

"Confidential Information" means all information of a confidential nature disclosed by one Party to the other in connection with this Agreement;

"Deliverables" means the outputs, reports, configurations, code, and other materials produced by the Provider in the course of providing the Services;

"Effective Date" means 1 April 2026;

"Initial Term" means 36 months from the Commencement Date (i.e. until 30 September 2028);

"Services" means the IT services described in Schedule 1;

"Service Levels" means the service level targets set out in Schedule 2;

"Working Day" means any day other than a Saturday, Sunday, or public holiday in England.

---

## 2. SERVICES

2.1 The Provider shall provide the Services to the Client during the Term with reasonable skill and care, in accordance with the Service Levels.

2.2 The Services shall include:

(a) management and maintenance of the Client's e-commerce platform (Magento/Adobe Commerce);

(b) cloud infrastructure management (AWS);

(c) application development and integration services;

(d) IT security monitoring and incident response;

(e) 24/7 helpdesk and technical support;

(f) data analytics and business intelligence support.

2.3 The Provider shall assign a dedicated team of no fewer than 8 full-time equivalent personnel to the Client's account.

---

## 3. FEES AND PAYMENT

3.1 The Client shall pay the Provider a monthly service fee of GBP 95,000 (ninety-five thousand pounds) exclusive of VAT for the Services described in Schedule 1.

3.2 Additional services outside the scope of Schedule 1 shall be charged at the following day rates:

| Role | Day Rate (excl. VAT) |
|------|---------------------|
| Technical Architect | GBP 1,400 |
| Senior Developer | GBP 1,100 |
| Developer | GBP 850 |
| Project Manager | GBP 950 |
| QA/Test Engineer | GBP 750 |

3.3 The Provider shall issue invoices monthly in advance for the service fee and monthly in arrears for any additional services.

3.4 **The Client shall pay each invoice within 30 (thirty) days of the date of the invoice.**

3.5 In the event of late payment, interest shall accrue at the rate of 4% per annum above the base rate of the Bank of England, calculated daily from the due date until the date of actual payment.

3.6 The Provider may suspend the Services upon giving 14 days' written notice if the Client's account is more than 60 days overdue, provided that the Provider shall use reasonable endeavours to minimise the impact of any suspension on the Client's operations.

---

## 4. SERVICE LEVELS AND REMEDIES

4.1 The Provider shall deliver the Services in accordance with the Service Levels set out in Schedule 2.

4.2 In the event that the Provider fails to meet any Service Level in any calendar month, the Client shall be entitled to a service credit calculated as follows:

| Service Level Achievement | Service Credit |
|--------------------------|----------------|
| 95% - 99.9% | 5% of monthly fee |
| 90% - 94.9% | 10% of monthly fee |
| Below 90% | 20% of monthly fee |

4.3 Service credits shall be capped at 20% of the monthly service fee per calendar month.

4.4 If the Provider fails to achieve 90% or above of any critical Service Level (being Platform Uptime and P1 Incident Response) in any 3 consecutive calendar months, the Client may terminate this Agreement by giving 30 days' written notice.

---

## 5. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

5.1 All intellectual property rights in the Provider's pre-existing technology, tools, frameworks, and methodologies ("Provider IP") shall remain the property of the Provider.

5.2 **All intellectual property rights in any Deliverables created by the Provider in the course of providing the Services, including all bespoke code, configurations, integrations, designs, and documentation, shall vest in the Client upon creation and payment in full.** The Provider hereby assigns (by way of present assignment of future rights) all such intellectual property rights to the Client with full title guarantee.

5.3 The Provider shall execute all documents and do all acts reasonably necessary to give effect to the assignment in clause 5.2.

5.4 Where Provider IP is incorporated into any Deliverable, the Provider grants the Client a perpetual, irrevocable, royalty-free, non-exclusive licence to use such Provider IP solely in connection with the Deliverables.

5.5 **The Client hereby assigns to the Provider all rights, title, and interest in any improvements, modifications, enhancements, or derivative works to the Provider's tools, frameworks, or methodologies that are conceived, developed, or first reduced to practice by the Client's employees or contractors in the course of working with the Provider under this Agreement, whether or not such improvements were made using the Client's data, specifications, or systems.** The Client shall ensure that its employees and contractors execute any assignments necessary to give effect to this clause.

5.6 **The Client shall not, and shall procure that its employees and contractors shall not, during the Term and for a period of 24 months following termination, directly or indirectly develop, commission, or procure any software, platform, or technology solution that is substantially similar to, or competitive with, any product or service offered by the Provider as at the date of termination.** For the avoidance of doubt, this restriction applies to the specific technology categories listed in Schedule 1 and any technology roadmap shared by the Provider with the Client during the Term.

## 6. LIABILITY

6.1 Nothing in this Agreement excludes or limits either Party's liability for death or personal injury caused by negligence, fraud, or fraudulent misrepresentation.

6.2 **Subject to clause 6.1, the Provider's total aggregate liability under or in connection with this Agreement, whether in contract, tort (including negligence), breach of statutory duty, or otherwise, shall not exceed an amount equal to 200% (two hundred per cent) of the total fees paid and payable by the Client under this Agreement in the 12-month period immediately preceding the date on which the claim arose.**

6.3 **Subject to clause 6.1, the Client's total aggregate liability under or in connection with this Agreement shall not exceed an amount equal to 200% (two hundred per cent) of the total fees paid and payable under this Agreement in the 12-month period immediately preceding the date on which the claim arose.**

6.4 Neither Party shall be liable for any indirect, consequential, or special loss, including loss of profit, loss of revenue, loss of data, loss of business opportunity, or loss of goodwill, except that this exclusion shall not apply to:

(a) the Client's obligation to pay the fees due under this Agreement;

(b) either Party's breach of clause 7 (Confidentiality);

(c) either Party's breach of clause 8 (Data Protection);

(d) the indemnity obligations in clause 5 (Intellectual Property).

---

## 7. CONFIDENTIALITY

7.1 Each Party shall keep confidential all Confidential Information of the other Party and shall not disclose it to any third party without the prior written consent of the disclosing Party.

7.2 The obligations of confidentiality shall not apply to information that:

(a) is or becomes publicly available through no fault of the receiving Party;

(b) was already known to the receiving Party prior to disclosure;

(c) is required to be disclosed by law or regulation.

7.3 The obligations of confidentiality shall survive the termination of this Agreement for a period of 5 years.

---

## 8. DATA PROTECTION

8.1 The Provider shall comply with all applicable data protection legislation, including the UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018, in the performance of the Services.

8.2 To the extent that the Provider processes personal data on behalf of the Client, the Parties shall comply with the Data Processing Agreement set out in Schedule 5.

---

## 9. TERMINATION

9.1 Either Party may terminate this Agreement immediately by written notice if the other Party:

(a) commits a material breach which is not remedied within 30 days of written notice specifying the breach;

(b) becomes insolvent, enters administration, has a receiver appointed, or passes a resolution for winding up.

9.2 **Either Party may terminate this Agreement for convenience by giving the other Party not less than 60 (sixty) days' written notice, such notice not to expire before the end of the 18th month following the Commencement Date** (i.e., the earliest effective termination date for convenience is 31 March 2027).

9.3 Following the Initial Term, this Agreement shall continue on a rolling 12-month basis and may be terminated by either Party giving 6 months' written notice.

9.4 In the event of termination by the Client under clause 4.4 (persistent Service Level failure) or clause 9.1(a) (Provider material breach), the Client shall not be liable for any fees in respect of the period following the effective date of termination.

9.5 Upon termination for any reason:

(a) the Provider shall provide transition assistance for a period of up to 6 months, the first 3 months of which shall be provided at no additional charge, and the remaining 3 months charged at 50% of the Provider's standard day rates;

(b) the Client shall pay all outstanding fees up to the effective date of termination;

(c) the Provider shall return or securely destroy all Client data within 30 days of the effective date of termination;

(d) the Provider shall provide the Client with a complete export of all Client data in a standard, machine-readable format (CSV, JSON, or SQL dump as specified by the Client).

---

## 10. FORCE MAJEURE

10.1 Neither Party shall be liable for any failure or delay in performing its obligations under this Agreement to the extent that such failure or delay is caused by a Force Majeure Event.

10.2 "Force Majeure Event" means any event beyond a Party's reasonable control, including acts of God, fire, flood, earthquake, epidemic, pandemic, war, terrorism, industrial action, or failure of third-party telecommunications or power supply.

10.3 If a Force Majeure Event continues for more than 60 consecutive days, either Party may terminate this Agreement by giving 30 days' written notice.

---

## 11. NON-SOLICITATION

11.1 During the Term and for a period of 12 months following termination, neither Party shall directly or indirectly solicit or entice away any employee of the other Party who has been involved in the performance or receipt of the Services, without the prior written consent of the other Party.

---

## 12. DISPUTE RESOLUTION

12.1 Any dispute arising under this Agreement shall first be referred to the Parties' respective account managers for resolution.

12.2 If not resolved within 20 Working Days, the dispute shall be escalated to a director of each Party.

12.3 If not resolved within a further 20 Working Days, either Party may refer the dispute to mediation under the CEDR Model Mediation Procedure.

12.4 If the dispute is not resolved by mediation within 60 days of referral, either Party may commence proceedings in the courts of England and Wales.

---

## 13. GENERAL

13.1 This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the Parties and supersedes the Original Agreement and all prior discussions and agreements.

13.2 No amendment shall be effective unless in writing and signed by both Parties.

13.3 The Provider may not sub-contract any material part of the Services without the Client's prior written consent (not to be unreasonably withheld).

13.4 This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of England and Wales.
